# Competitive Mapping

Point solutions solve a moment in time. IntegrateWise creates continuity across time.

This doc is for sales conversations only. Do not use competitor names in advertising.

---

## Continuity vs. Point Solutions

| Dimension   | What they sell                         | What IntegrateWise delivers                          |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Timeframe   | Today’s workflow                       | Today’s workflow plus tomorrow’s memory              |
| Memory      | Last session, within one tool          | Governed memory across tools, users, and AI sessions |
| Trust       | Confidence scores or session summaries | Evidence-backed proposals with approval gates        |
| Control     | Ask-to-act                             | Ask-to-assign-to-approve                             |
| Portability | Context trapped in their product       | Truth that travels with your organization            |
| Governance  | Afterthought or absent                 | Pre-proposal and post-proposal gates by design       |

## Objection Patterns by Category

### Automation Tools

**They say:** “Connect your apps, automate your workflow.”

**The gap:** They move data between apps. They do not remember why the data matters, what happened before it arrived, or what should happen next.

**Our frame:** IntegrateWise connects context, not just apps.

---

### AI Chat / Copilot

**They say:** “Ask your data anything.”

**The gap:** Every conversation starts from zero. Memory is session-based. Actions are proposed, not governed. Answers are grounded in one source, not assembled across sources.

**Our frame:** Every AI remembers. Only IntegrateWise remembers the truth.

---

### Internal Dashboards

**They say:** “We built this exactly for us.”

**The gap:** Static reports do not propose, follow through, or learn. Maintenance is forever. Engineer turnover is the biggest risk to continuity.

**Our frame:** Dashboards show data. IntegrateWise tells you what to do about it—and captures institutional memory permanently.

---

### CS Platforms

**They say:** “One health score, one view for customer success.”

**The gap:** Rules require manual configuration. Health scores live inside the CRM. The AI does not reason over conversations, support signals, billing state, and usage together.

**Our frame:** IntegrateWise feeds better data into your CS platform and catches signals across the whole stack.

---

### CDPs

**They say:** “Unified customer profiles for marketing.”

**The gap:** Audience resolution for ads and segmentation. Not operational context for decisions, approvals, and governed execution.

**Our frame:** CDPs optimize your marketing. IntegrateWise optimizes your operations.

## Continuity Framing in Closes

Use these lines when the prospect is comparing one-use-case tools:

> “Every tool you evaluated solves a moment in time. IntegrateWise is the layer that makes those moments part of a continuous story.”

> “You do not choose between a better dashboard and a better AI. You choose the infrastructure that makes every dashboard and every AI better because they share the same truth.”

> “Implementation timelines shift when you compare point solutions. Continuity compounds.”

## Positioning Checklist

Before any competitive conversation, confirm:

- [ ] I am comparing categories, not mocking brands.
- [ ] I am leading with their pain, not our feature.
- [ ] I have named one measurable outcome, not ten abstract benefits.
- [ ] I have anchored the brand doctrine once.
- [ ] I left them with a next step that proves the promise, not a deck that explains it.
