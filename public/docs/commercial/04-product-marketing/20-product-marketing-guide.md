## Purpose

The operating manual for the product marketing function: what PMM owns, the launch tiering system, asset standards, and the audit cadences that keep the whole Commercial OS consistent. In a solo-founder company, "PMM" is a hat — this guide makes the hat wearable in two hours a week.

**Target audience:** Whoever wears the PMM hat (founder now; first marketing hire later).

## 2. The Message Discipline (PMM's first law)

Nothing ships — page, post, deck, email sequence, UI string — unless it can answer:

1. Which **failure** does it name? (fragmentation / amnesia / anarchy)
2. Which **pillar** does it advance? (One Workspace / Twin on the Bench / Truth You Own)
3. What **proof** does it carry within two sentences of its claim?
4. Does its vocabulary pass [18 §6](../03-positioning-messaging/18-terminology-guide.md) for its audience?

## 3. Launch Tiering

| Tier                       | What qualifies                                          | Motions                                                                                                                                     | Example                                                                         |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **T1 — Narrative launch**  | New category-level capability or company milestone      | Manifesto/essay + press ([65](../12-corporate/65-press-kit.md)) + launch page + founder posts ×5 + newsletter + webinar + sales deck update | Public launch ([51](../09-gtm/51-launch-strategy.md)); Twin generally available |
| **T2 — Capability launch** | New workbench, major Twin capability, marquee connector | Blog post + founder post ×2 + changelog + demo update + battle-card refresh                                                                 | Sales workbench; Salesforce connector                                           |
| **T3 — Release note**      | Improvements, minor connectors                          | Changelog + newsletter mention                                                                                                              | New sync frequency                                                              |

**T1/T2 launch checklist:** narrative written before the build finishes (if we can't say why it matters, question the build) → terminology check → demo updated → sales enabled _before_ public ([71](../13-operations/71-internal-enablement.md)) → proof point identified → metrics instrumented ([73](../13-operations/73-marketing-metrics.md)) → retro within 2 weeks.

## 4. Asset Standards & Voice Audit

**Every external asset carries:** one failure named, one pillar advanced, one proof, one next step. No asset tries to say everything — that's what the website's architecture is for ([52](../10-website/52-website-strategy.md)).

**Quarterly voice audit (2-hour ritual):** sample 10 live assets + 20 UI strings → score against [13 Brand Story §2 voice table](../01-foundation/13-brand-story.md) → log violations → fix top 5 → record score trend. Target ≥ 95% pass.

**Claims register:** every quantitative public claim (connector count, "<200ms", "only approval-gated memory") lives in the [79 Appendix claims table](../14-appendix/79-appendix.md) with verification date. Unverified claims don't ship.

## 5. Evidence Pipeline (pre-customer stage)

Until named case studies exist, proof comes from four honest sources, always labeled:

1. **Design-partner metrics** — "design partners reduced QBR prep 4 hrs → 12 min" (attributed as design partner).
2. **Architecture demonstrations** — the approval gate and provenance panel _are_ proof; demo them, don't claim them.
3. **Founder origin** — the $8M story, told as story ([14](../01-foundation/14-founder-story.md)).
4. **Live product facts** — 70+ connectors, Entity360 <200ms, verified against the claims register.

Rule: never let scarcity of proof inflate the proof we have. Understated and verifiable beats impressive and fragile — our brand _is_ evidence discipline.

**KPIs:** voice audit score ≥95%; launch checklist completion rate 100% for T1/T2; claims register freshness ≤90 days; message pull-through in win/loss ([73 Marketing Metrics](../13-operations/73-marketing-metrics.md)).
