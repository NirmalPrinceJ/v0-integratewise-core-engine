# 25 — Twin Story

Status: CANONICAL · Commercial OS
Owner: Product Marketing
Last verified: 2026-07-21

---

## Purpose

How to tell the Twin — the concept most at risk of being misheard as "chatbot." This story exists to make the difference unmissable.

**Definition (verbatim):** _A person's governed AI counterpart that works alongside them on the same workbench — it prepares, suggests, and proposes; it never owns truth and never acts without approval._

---

## 1. The Story

Everyone has met the other kind of AI. It lives in a tab. It knows nothing about your morning until you paste your morning into it. It answers with confidence, sometimes about things that aren't true. It forgets you by tomorrow — or worse, remembers something it invented about you. You learned its real place: emails, summaries, nothing that matters. The quiet demotion.

The Twin is built from the opposite premises. It is **yours** — one Twin per person, scoped by your role and authority, at your bench. It **already knows the context**, because it shares your workbench and reads the same Spine of truth you do — it never asks you to re-explain your own work. It **prepares the hard parts**: the meeting brief before you open it, the analysis behind the risk signal, the draft grounded in what was actually said. It **shows its evidence** — every claim links to sources; and when it has nothing grounded to offer, **it is silent**. No filler, no vibes, no invented renewal dates.

And the line it cannot cross, by architecture: **the Twin proposes; you approve.** It cannot write to organizational memory — TruthLayer requires your approval. It cannot execute a consequential action — governance requires your approval. It is a colleague with excellent preparation and no authority — which is exactly why you can finally give it consequential work.

> The experience promise, verbatim: _"My work already knows its context. AI prepared the hard parts. I decide."_

## 2. What the Twin Does (grammar, not feature list)

`NOTICE` what changed and matters · `EXPLAIN` this work from its context · `RELATE` this to what the org knows · `PREPARE` the brief/analysis/draft · `SUGGEST` the next step · `PROPOSE` the reviewable action.

And what it never does (doctrine): own truth or memory · own identity, policy, or authorization · execute unapproved · occupy a permanent chat destination that competes with the work.

## 3. The Hard Distinctions (say these before the prospect asks)

| They think                     | The correction                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "So it's a chatbot on my data" | A chatbot is a _destination_ — you go to it and narrate. The Twin is at the bench: its help lands inside the work, grounded, with evidence.                                                                       |
| "Like a copilot?"              | Copilots are sidecars to one app and see that app. Your Twin works over your organization's assembled truth — every connected system, one context. "Copilots visit your apps. IntegrateWise is where work lives." |
| "An autonomous agent then"     | The opposite bet. Agents seek autonomy; the Twin's contract is preparation + proposal, with your approval gating anything real. That contract is _why_ it gets real work.                                         |
| "Will it replace my team?"     | It's one-per-person, not one-instead-of-person. The accountable human is the design center — the Twin amplifies them.                                                                                             |

## 4. Trust Mechanics (why belief is not required)

Evidence chains on interventions · TruthLayer approval on memory · action approval + audit trail · confidence honesty (silence over speculation) · model-agnostic (swap the AI; the Twin's knowledge stays, because knowledge lives in _your_ Spine).

**Trust line, verbatim:** "Every AI remembers. Only IntegrateWise remembers the truth."

## 5. Language Rules

- Always "your Twin" (possessive, singular, capital T). Never "the AI," "the bot," "our assistant," "agents."
- Verbs of preparation and proposal, never verbs of autonomous completion ("your Twin drafted" ✓ · "your Twin handled it" ✗).
- The human decides in every sentence where something consequential happens.

**KPIs:** Approved Twin Actions per seat/week (north star); intervention acceptance rate; "chatbot" misreadings in discovery (should trend to zero as messaging lands) ([73](../13-operations/73-marketing-metrics.md)).
