## Purpose

The defensible differences — what only IntegrateWise can claim, why competitors structurally can't follow, and how each difference converts to a sales argument. This is the moat register; [09 Battle Cards](../02-category-market/09-competitive-battle-cards.md) applies it per competitor.

## 1. The Differentiation Stack (five moats, evolved from the investor canon)

### Moat 1 — Shared-State Collaboration (the category moat)

**What:** Human and Twin work on the _same_ workbench over the _same_ live state. Not chat beside work — collaboration inside work. The Twin's interventions (`NOTICE → PROPOSE`) land in the semantic slot of the work itself.

**Why others can't follow:** Copilots are architecturally sidecars to a host app; assistants are architecturally conversation threads. Neither has a shared work-state model to collaborate _on_. Rebuilding around one means abandoning their core UX.

**Sales argument:** "Ask where their AI's output lives. If the answer is 'in the chat,' you're looking at a sidecar, not a colleague."

### Moat 2 — TruthLayer: Approval-Gated Memory (the trust moat)

**What:** AI proposes memory; humans approve it; only then does it become organizational knowledge. Every other AI auto-writes memory — it guesses, and its guesses compound.

**Why others can't follow:** Auto-memory is their engagement engine; gating it degrades their core metric. For us, gating _is_ the product philosophy.

**Sales argument:** "ChatGPT remembers what it wants. IntegrateWise remembers what you approve."

### Moat 3 — The Adaptive Spine: Truth Outside the Apps (the data moat)

**What:** Canonical operational state independent of any SaaS vendor, with per-field provenance and confidence. Tools stay; truth is projected to workbenches by the Projection Engine.

**Why others can't follow:** Every incumbent's truth is its own silo — Salesforce's truth is Salesforce. A vendor whose business is the silo cannot sell truth-above-the-silo without cannibalizing itself.

**Sales argument:** "Your CRM's truth is the CRM. Your organization's truth should belong to your organization."

### Moat 4 — Governance as Architecture (the enterprise moat)

**What:** Proposal → approval → execution → audit is the physics of the system. Fail-closed boundaries; identity-bound, tenant-scoped, policy-checked exchanges; the platform cannot be talked out of governance by a clever prompt.

**Why others can't follow:** Agent vendors race toward autonomy as their differentiator; retrofitting mandatory human gates reverses their pitch. Regulation is moving toward us, not them.

**Sales argument:** "Their roadmap removes the human. Ours arms the human."

### Moat 5 — Compounding Continuity (the time moat)

**What:** Every approved fact, decision, and handover enriches the Spine. Value compounds with tenure; switching cost is accumulated institutional intelligence — earned, not imposed.

**Why others can't follow:** Session-based tools reset by design. Continuity requires the other four moats first.

**Sales argument:** the continuity math — "Month 1: one week of memory replaces three status meetings… Month 12: institutional memory no longer leaves with the people."

## 2. Differentiation Claims — Approved Wording

| Claim                                                                      | Status              | Guard-rail                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Only workspace where humans and AI collaborate on shared, governed state" | Approved            | Keep "governed" — ungoverned shared canvases exist                                                        |

| "AI never owns organizational truth"                                       | Approved (doctrine) | Architecture claim — demo the write path                                                                  |
| "Swap AI models without losing anything"                                   | Approved            | Provider-agnostic architecture; demo config switch                                                        |
| "Nothing executes without approval"                                        | Approved            | Always pair with audit-trail view                                                                         |
| "70+ connectors"                                                           | Approved            | Update count quarterly from live registry                                                                 |

Forbidden differentiation: price ("cheaper than Gainsight" invites race-to-bottom), model quality ("better AI" — we rent models like everyone), connector count as primary story (reads iPaaS).

## 3. The Differentiation Test Grid

Use in deal reviews. If we can't check ≥3, we're in the wrong deal or told the story wrong.

- [ ] Prospect can restate why a Twin ≠ a copilot (shared bench vs. sidecar).
- [ ] Prospect saw the approval gate and audit trail live.
- [ ] Prospect understands truth lives in _their_ Spine, exportable, provider-agnostic.
- [ ] Prospect's security/IT acknowledged governance-by-architecture.
- [ ] Prospect articulated what compounds by month 12.

**KPIs:** win rate in deals where ≥3 boxes checked vs. <3 (expect >2x gap); competitive-loss reasons tagged to a moat (any moat losing >30% of its matchups triggers repositioning review).
