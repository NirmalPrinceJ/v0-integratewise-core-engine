# 46 — Expansion Strategy

Status: CANONICAL · Commercial OS
Owner: Founder (CS + sales)
Last verified: 2026-07-21

---

## Purpose

How land becomes expand: the three expansion vectors, their triggers, and the plays that convert earned trust into ARR without ever feeling like upsell. Expansion is architecturally native here — same Spine, new benches — and the strategy's job is to harvest what the architecture plants.

**Target audience:** CS (trigger detection), sales (expansion deals), founder (NRR planning).

---

## 1. The Expansion Model

NRR target ≥ 120% built from three vectors (mirrors the packaging ladder [31](../05-value-pricing/31-packaging-strategy.md)):

| Vector                               | Motion                                             | Typical uplift                   |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **V1 Seats** — same department grows | New CSMs/AMs onboard into existing benches         | +10–20%/yr organic               |
| **V2 Tiers** — trust matures         | Growth → Command (the approval-and-memory upgrade) | ~2x on upgraded seats            |
| **V3 Benches** — new departments     | Sales, RevOps, Exec workbenches on the same Spine  | The compounding vector: +50–200% |

**The structural advantage (say it to buyers):** expansion isn't a new implementation. The Spine already holds the organization's truth; a new department is a new _projection_ of it. "Turning on the Sales workbench" is closer to adding a seat than buying software — day-one value inherits all accumulated truth and memory.

## 2. Trigger Catalog (CS detects; logged weekly [44 §2](44-customer-success-playbook.md))

**V1 seat triggers:** new-hire announcements in the account team · login-shared-screen smells (two names, one seat — convert gently) · manager asking for "a view for the new person."

**V2 tier triggers (Growth accounts):** suggestions frequently executed _manually_ in tools (approving in spirit — the Command pitch writes itself) · TruthLayer questions ("can it remember that we decided…?") · security/audit asks that map to Command governance features.

**V3 bench triggers (the big ones):**

- **The forwarded morning:** a CS workbench screenshot lands in a sales/exec Slack — "what's that?" (Ask champions to tell us when it happens; it always happens.)
- **The QBR guest:** a sales leader or COO attends a QBR where CS presents Twin-prepared, evidence-chained material ("why doesn't my team have this?")
- **The handover proof:** an account transfers between CSMs with full continuity; the VP notices what turnover used to cost
- **The catch that crossed departments:** an expansion signal CS surfaced becomes sales pipeline — sales just experienced the product's value without owning it

## 3. Expansion Plays

**Play V2 — "You're already approving in spirit" (CS-led, 2 weeks)**
Evidence: pull 5 examples where the team manually executed what the Twin suggested → show the delta (same judgment, plus the gate, execution, and receipt) → 14-day Command trial on those exact workflows → decision at trial review with realized numbers. Convert rate target ≥ 50%.

**Play V3 — The Second Bench (CS opens, sales closes, 30–45 days)**

1. Champion brief: arm P2/P3 with the internal case (their own realized-value review — [64 template](../12-corporate/64-case-study-templates.md) internal variant)
2. Cross-department demo: _their_ data already in the Spine — demo the Sales workbench on accounts the CRM already connected. Recognition is instant; this is our highest-converting demo ([39](../07-sales/39-demo-guide.md) variant)
3. Scoped land: 10–15 seats in the new department, own success criteria ([45](45-onboarding-playbook.md) runs again, faster — hydration already done)
4. Commercial: new order form at list (design-partner discount does _not_ auto-extend to new departments — [30 §3](../05-value-pricing/30-pricing-strategy.md))

**Play V3-Exec — The Enterprise Conversation (founder-led)**
Trigger: 2+ departments live or P5 engaged. The pitch elevates from department outcomes to organizational continuity: one Spine across CS+Sales+Ops, exec workbench, Enterprise tier (SSO, policy, SLA). This is where ACV crosses $75K ([30 deal math](../05-value-pricing/30-pricing-strategy.md)).

## 4. Rules

- **Expansion follows evidence, never precedes it.** No V3 play before the wedge's day-90 realized-value review exists. Premature expansion burns the champion's credibility — and ours.
- **CS opens, sales closes, one story.** The expansion deck is the customer's own numbers; sales adds commercial structure ([37 Play 7](../07-sales/37-sales-playbook.md)).
- **Never call it upsell in front of the customer.** The language: "extending what's already working" — because architecturally, it is.
- **Instrument the triggers.** "Forwarded morning" and "QBR guest" moments get logged when detected; they're leading NRR indicators ([75](../13-operations/75-customer-success-metrics.md)).

**KPIs:** NRR ≥ 120% (at scale; design-partner cohort tracked separately) · V2 conversion ≥ 40% of Growth accounts within 12 mo ([31 §4](../05-value-pricing/31-packaging-strategy.md)) · V3: ≥ 30% of customers add a second bench within 12 mo · expansion ARR ≥ 40% of new ARR by end-2027 ([77](../13-operations/77-annual-gtm-plan.md)) · trigger-to-play conversion tracked per play.
