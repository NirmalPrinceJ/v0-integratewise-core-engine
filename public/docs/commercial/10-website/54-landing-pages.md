## Purpose

The campaign-specific landing pages beyond the core site ([53](53-website-copy.md)): each with a single job, a single audience, a single CTA, and complete copy. Landing pages are arguments, not brochures.

**Target audience:** PMM, whoever builds pages; sales (for send-able links).

## LP-1: The Manifesto / Category Page (`/manifesto`)

**Job:** convert the intellectually curious into believers and subscribers. Traffic: founder content, PR, launch ([51 Layer 1](../09-gtm/51-launch-strategy.md)).

# Work is leaving the apps.

Software unbundled work into a hundred silos — and people became the glue. Then AI arrived, bolted onto each silo, and made it worse: a copilot per app, a chat tab per task, memory that guesses. Most people quietly demoted AI to writing emails.

The next era doesn't add another tool. It gives work a home.

**A workspace for human and AI collaborative work** is a system where people and governed AI counterparts do work together on shared surfaces, over canonical operational state the organization owns, with human approval on every consequential action.

Five requirements define it — grade any vendor, including us:
1. **Shared work surface** — human and AI on the same live work, not a chat beside it.
2. **Owned canonical state** — truth across all tools, held by the organization, with provenance.
3. **Governed collaboration** — AI proposes; humans approve; everything audits.
4. **Persistent continuity** — context survives sessions, tools, people, and model swaps.
5. **Capability execution** — approved actions actually run in connected systems.

**CTA:** *Get the weekly essay* → newsletter ([60](../11-marketing/60-newsletter-strategy.md)) · secondary: *See the workspace →*

## LP-5: The Comparison Pages (`/vs/copilots`, `/vs/cs-platforms`, `/vs/assistants`)

**Job:** capture comparison-intent search ([55](55-seo-strategy.md)) without naming competitors (advertising rule [09](../02-category-market/09-competitive-battle-cards.md) preserved — pages compare *categories*).

Template (each ~600 words):
1. **Respect block:** what that category is genuinely good at (documents / CS workflow / drafting).
2. **The boundary:** the job it structurally can't do — the silo, the rules engine, the amnesia — told through one concrete scenario (the Meridian pattern).
3. **The rubric:** the five category requirements, self-graded honestly.
4. **The one-liner:** the canonical kill line for that category ([18 §4](../03-positioning-messaging/18-terminology-guide.md)).

**CTA:** *See the difference in one morning →*

## LP-6: The POV Application Page (`/proof`)

**Job:** convert high-intent evaluators into scoped POVs — self-qualification built in.

# Thirty days. Your tools. Three numbers. Then you decide.

A proof of value, not a free trial: up to 10 seats, full capability, your real stack (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zendesk, Stripe…), three success criteria we agree in writing — like *morning prep under 10 minutes* and *one documented catch on a real account* — and a decision date.

**Form fields:** name · work email · company · CS team size (dropdown: <5 / 5–15 / 15–50 / 50+) · CRM (HubSpot / Salesforce / other) · "what surprised you last quarter?" (free text — discovery starts here).

**CTA:** *Apply for a proof of value*

## Landing Page Standards

One job, one CTA per page (secondary CTA allowed only if it serves the same visitor) · headline names the visitor's state, not our product · every page passes the terminology checklist ([18 §8](../03-positioning-messaging/18-terminology-guide.md)) and claims register ([79](../14-appendix/79-appendix.md)) · UTM-instrumented; conversion tracked per page in [73 Marketing Metrics](../13-operations/73-marketing-metrics.md) · pages that convert < 2% for two months get rewritten or retired.

**KPIs:** conversion per LP (targets: LP-1 subscribe ≥ 8% · LP-2 demo ≥ 5% · LP-3 calculator ≥ 10%, POV ≥ 3% · LP-6 application completion ≥ 40%) · ICP share of conversions ≥ 60% · sales usage of LPs in sequences.
