# 55 — SEO Strategy

Status: CANONICAL · Commercial OS
Owner: PMM hat

## Purpose

How IntegrateWise wins search — with a stage-honest strategy: we cannot outrank incumbents on head terms today, so we own the terms we're creating, dominate the long-tail pain queries our ICP actually types, and build authority through the founder's narrative engine.

**Target audience:** PMM, content writers, site builders.

---

## 1. Strategy: Three Rings

**Ring 1 — Category terms (own them from day one).** Nobody ranks for our vocabulary yet because we're coining it: *workspace for human and AI collaborative work* · *human AI collaborative work* · *shared workbench* · *AI twin for work* · *governed AI memory* · *approval-gated AI* · *adaptive spine*. Every category page, manifesto, and essay reinforces these. Winning searches that don't exist yet is cheap; when the category conversation starts (and giants will start it — [07 §3](../02-category-market/07-industry-landscape.md)), we're the definitional result. Track pickup monthly; this ring is the launch's true north ([51 §4](../09-gtm/51-launch-strategy.md)).

**Ring 2 — Pain queries (the ICP's actual searches).** High-intent long-tail where incumbents write generic listicles and we write the definitive answer from lived pain:

| Cluster | Example queries | Asset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The morning ritual | "too many tools customer success," "CSM spends hours gathering data," "reduce QBR prep time" | CS pillar essay + LP-3 ([54](54-landing-pages.md)) |
| Churn signals | "detect churn signals early," "churn surprised us," "customer health score didn't work" | Wound-story essays; signal-evidence explainers |
| AI trust | "AI hallucinations at work," "can I trust AI with customer data," "AI forgot context," "govern AI memory" | Demotion essays; governance explainers; LP-2 |
| Comparison intent | "Gainsight alternatives," "copilot vs [category]," "AI for account management" | /vs pages ([54 LP-5](54-landing-pages.md)) — category-compared, never brand-attacked |
| Stack-specific | "HubSpot account 360," "connect HubSpot Zendesk Stripe one view" | Connector/solution pages; marketplace listings ([49](../09-gtm/49-marketplace-strategy.md)) |

**Ring 3 — Authority (E-E-A-T through the founder).** Search engines increasingly reward demonstrated experience; our founder *is* the experience: MuleSoft architect + CSM bylines on every essay, the $8M story as the About backbone, design-partner numbers with methodology notes. Founder-led content ([57](../11-marketing/57-founder-led-marketing.md)) is the authority engine; the site is where it accrues.

## 2. Site Mechanics

Technical hygiene as table stakes: static-fast (<1s p75 — also a brand claim [52 §4](52-website-strategy.md)) · clean semantic structure · schema markup (Organization, Product, FAQ on security/pricing pages) · every essay has one target query cluster, stated in the doc's frontmatter before writing (no query, no essay — prevents content drift) · internal linking follows the hub model: essays → pillar pages → product pages (mirrors the Commercial OS's own cross-linking).

**Content cadence (realistic for founder + PMM hat):** 1 essay/week ([56](../11-marketing/56-content-strategy.md) owns the calendar) · each essay shipped natively to LinkedIn + newsletter, canonical on site · quarterly: refresh top-10 performing pages; prune or merge sub-100-visit pages.

## 3. What We Don't Do (stage discipline)

No paid search until LTV evidence ([50 §3](../09-gtm/50-gtm-strategy.md)) · no programmatic SEO farms (a thousand thin connector pages would rank briefly and poison the brand's evidence-first posture) · no guest-post link buying · no chasing head terms ("AI workspace" solo is a bonfire of effort pre-authority — we get it via Ring 1 as the category grows).

**KPIs:** Ring 1 — category-term impressions + our rank (target: #1 on exact category phrase by launch+90) · Ring 2 — non-brand organic visits/mo (500 by launch+90, 2,500 by mid-2027) and organic → CTA conversion ≥ 3% · Ring 3 — branded search volume trend; essay-attributed pipeline in [73 Marketing Metrics](../13-operations/73-marketing-metrics.md).
