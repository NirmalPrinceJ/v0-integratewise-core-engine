# Your work, already assembled.

Every morning, your Shared Workbench opens on three things: **what changed** overnight across your connected tools, **what needs you** today, and **what your Twin already prepared.** No tabs. No searching. No reconstruction.

## Composed for your role — projected from one truth.

A workbench isn't a dashboard with your name on it. It's a working surface composed from your role, your active focus, and your organization's canonical state. A CS workbench carries accounts, health, and commitments. A sales workbench carries pipeline and deal signals. Same truth underneath — the Adaptive Spine — different bench for every job.

**Dashboards show work. Workbenches do work.** Read the account. See the signal and its evidence. Draft the response with your Twin. Approve it. Watch it execute through your real tools — without leaving the bench.

- **Assign Your Twin** — delegate the preparation: briefs, analyses, drafts.
- **Approve Twin's Action** — authorize a reviewable action across your connected tools.

Everything else is your work, arranged around you.

## PAGE: Product / The Adaptive Spine

# Your organization's truth. Finally yours.

Every tool you use keeps its own version of reality. The CRM has one Acme Corp. Support has another. Billing, a third. The real story — where the usage dip, the ticket spike, and the budget email are one story — exists nowhere.

The Adaptive Spine is where it exists now.

## One entity. Every source. Full receipts.
The Spine ingests from your connected tools, resolves duplicates across systems, and assembles each account, contact, and deal into one canonical record. Click any field: its source system, its timestamp, its confidence. Board decks with sources, not vibes.

## Memory with a gate.
AI can propose knowledge into your Spine. It cannot write it. Every addition to organizational memory passes a human approval — so your company's memory contains only what someone verified. **Every AI remembers. Only IntegrateWise remembers the truth.**

## Owned, not hosted.
Your Spine is yours: export it anytime, in open formats. Swap AI providers by configuration — nothing resets, because knowledge lives in your Spine, not in a vendor's model. **Truth you own. AI you rent. Approval in between.**

## PAGE: Product / Governance & Trust

# The AI your security team will approve. Literally.

IntegrateWise doesn't ask for trust. It removes the need for it.

**Memory is gated.** AI proposes; a human approves; only then does knowledge enter your organization's memory.
**Action is gated.** Proposal → review → approval → execution → audit. The only path. An AI message is never an approval.
**Everything is attributed.** Every fact has a source. Every proposal has evidence. Every approval has a name. Every execution has a result.
**Boundaries fail closed.** Every exchange is identity-bound, tenant-scoped, policy-checked, and time-bounded. When a check can't complete, the answer is no. A clever prompt cannot talk the platform out of policy.
**Nobody is above it.** Not the model. Not an admin shortcut. Not us.

## Why this makes AI *more* useful, not less.
Teams give consequential work to AI they can supervise. The approval gate isn't the tax on the Twin's power — it's the reason your team finally lets the Twin do real work.
