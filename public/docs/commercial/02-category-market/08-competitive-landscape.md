## Purpose

Who we actually meet in deals, how each rival's story works, and where each one structurally breaks against the category rubric. Battle-ready per-competitor material lives in [09 Battle Cards](09-competitive-battle-cards.md); this document is the strategic map.

**Target audience:** Sales, marketing, product, founders.

---

## 1. The Real Competitive Order (by deal frequency)

1. **Status quo / do nothing** — the Human API endures because it's free-looking. Beat with quantified pain ([29 ROI](../05-value-pricing/29-roi-framework.md)).
2. **Internal builds** — "we made a dashboard." Beat with maintenance truth + intelligence gap.
3. **General assistants** (ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini in a tab) — "we already use AI." Beat with the Four Enemies.
4. **Suite copilots** (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Gemini for Workspace) — "it's included." Beat with cross-silo truth + governance.
5. **CS platforms** (Gainsight, Totango) — wedge-market incumbent. Beat with cross-stack signals + no rules engine.
6. **Record-vendor agents** (Salesforce Agentforce, HubSpot Breeze) — "our CRM does AI now." Beat with above-the-silo neutrality.
7. **Search/knowledge AI** (Glean, Notion AI) — "we can find everything." Beat with state vs. documents; action vs. answers.
8. **Automation agents** (Zapier, Workato, n8n agents) — rarely head-to-head; category confusion risk. Reframe, then partner.

**The pattern:** everyone has a fragment. Nobody else has owned truth + mandatory approval — the two requirements that conflict with every incumbent's business model (silo defense or engagement economics). That's the structural read: our moats are their strategy taxes.

## 3. Competitive Postures

| Versus          | Posture                                                                  | One line                                                                     |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Suites          | **Coexist** — we're the operational layer over the stack they're part of | "Keep Copilot for documents. IntegrateWise is where your _operations_ live." |
| Record vendors  | **Complement now, category-compete later**                               | "Salesforce is one of the tools we connect."                                 |
| Model vendors   | **Suppliers** — provider-agnostic by doctrine                            | "Truth you own. AI you rent."                                                |
| CS platforms    | **Displace in wedge** (or feed, when entrenched)                         | "We catch what it can't see — signals across the whole stack."               |
| Automation      | **Partner** — execution targets for governed handoff                     | "Your Zapier gets smarter instructions."                                     |
| Assistants      | **Reframe** — different job entirely                                     | "That's a chat tab. This is where the work lives."                           |
| Internal builds | **Respect, then retire**                                                 | "What happens when its engineer leaves?"                                     |

## 4. Standing Intelligence Discipline

- Quarterly: refresh scorecard, re-verify "only approval-gated memory" claim, update battle cards; log competitor moves against [07 scenarios](07-industry-landscape.md).
- Every loss tagged to a competitor + moat in CRM ([74 Sales Metrics](../13-operations/74-sales-metrics.md)); any moat losing >30% of matchups triggers repositioning review ([17](../03-positioning-messaging/17-differentiation.md)).
- Never name competitors in advertising; sales conversations only (standing rule).

**KPIs:** competitive win rate by rival; % deals with no competitor (category creation working); scorecard freshness ≤ 90 days.
