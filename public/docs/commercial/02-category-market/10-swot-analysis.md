## Cross-Reading (the strategic sentences)

- **S1+O2:** our hardest architectural choices are becoming procurement requirements — governance is a tailwind only for us.
- **W1+W5:** the biggest risk is not competition; it's building breadth before one person feels one workflow lighten. The build plan's discipline _is_ the strategy.
- **S2+W3:** until proof exists, the founder story carries the trust load — invest in telling it well, everywhere.
- **T1+S5:** when giants adopt the language, the category wins and the rubric decides who owns it. Prepare the rubric now.

**KPIs:** quarterly SWOT refresh completed; every W and T has a live mitigation with owner; tripwire monitoring in place (calendar + alerts).
