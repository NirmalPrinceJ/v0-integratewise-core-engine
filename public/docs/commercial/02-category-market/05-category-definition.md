## Purpose

Define the category we intend to create and lead: its name, boundaries, requirements, and the rules for defending it. Category definition is offense — whoever writes the category's dictionary grades every vendor in it.

**Target audience:** Analysts, press, investors, senior prospects; internally, everyone who frames the company.

---

## 1. The Category

> **Workspace for Human and AI Collaborative Work**

Short forms: "the collaborative work workspace" · "the Workspace" (in context). Evolution ledger and forbidden alternates: [18 Terminology §1](../03-positioning-messaging/18-terminology-guide.md).

**Definition (the sentence analysts should repeat):**

> A workspace for human and AI collaborative work is a system where people and governed AI counterparts do work together on shared surfaces, over canonical operational state the organization owns, with human approval and audit on every consequential action.

---

## 2. Why a New Category (and not a niche of an old one)

Existing categories structurally cannot hold the product:

| Adjacent category            | Why we don't fit                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| AI assistant / copilot       | Assistants converse beside work; we share state inside work. No collaboration model, no owned truth.               |
| iPaaS / integration platform | Moves data without meaning, memory, or governance. We connect _context_, not just apps.                            |
| BI / dashboards              | Read-only yesterday. Workbenches are read-write operational surfaces. "Dashboards show work. Workbenches do work." |
| Knowledge base / wiki        | Stores what people write. The Spine captures what _happens_, with provenance.                                      |
| CS platform / CRM add-on     | One department, one system of record. We're role-composed across all departments, above all systems.               |
| Agent platform               | Races toward autonomy. We architect for governed collaboration — the opposite bet.                                 |

A product that is mispositioned into any of these loses: priced like iPaaS, evaluated like a chatbot, or dismissed as another dashboard. Category creation is not vanity — it's the pricing and evaluation frame.

---

## 3. Category Requirements (the grading rubric we publish)

To qualify as a Workspace for Human and AI Collaborative Work, a system must have **all five**:

1. **Shared work surface** — human and AI operate on the same live work object, not a chat beside it.
2. **Canonical operational state** — truth assembled across the org's tools, owned by the organization, with provenance.
3. **Governed collaboration** — AI proposes; humans approve; every consequential action is gated and audited.
4. **Persistent continuity** — context survives sessions, tools, personnel, and AI-model changes.
5. **Capability execution** — approved actions actually execute across connected systems (not advice-only).

The rubric is our analyst artifact ([66](../12-corporate/66-analyst-relations.md)) and evaluation checklist for buyers ([43 Procurement FAQ](../07-sales/43-procurement-faq.md)). Scoring competitors against it: [08 Competitive Landscape](08-competitive-landscape.md). By construction and honest audit, only IntegrateWise currently meets all five — requirement 2 (owned truth with provenance) and 3 (mandatory approval) are the fork most vendors cannot take.

## 4. Category Narrative (why now — compressed from [04](../01-foundation/04-long-term-narrative.md))

Fragmentation made the person the integration layer. Bolted-on AI made fragmentation worse and burned trust (~80% quiet demotion). Three forces make the recentering inevitable now: the measurable Empty Context Tax, protocol + edge-infrastructure maturity (MCP, serverless edge), and institutional demand for human oversight (76% of enterprises). The category is the shape of the resolution: work recenters into one workspace; truth consolidates under organizational ownership; AI joins the bench under governance.

## 5. Category Strategy & Defense

**Naming discipline:** we always say the full category name in first mention of every asset; shorthand after. We never accept "AI workspace" (ambiguous, model-vendor-friendly) or "agentic workspace" (autonomy frame) as substitutes.

**Category creation plan:**

| Phase                 | Move                                                                                                                                | Artifact                                                                                                               |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Now (design partners) | Manifesto + rubric published; founder-led narrative                                                                                 | Manifesto post ([56](../11-marketing/56-content-strategy.md)), category page ([54](../10-website/54-landing-pages.md)) |
| Launch                | Name the category in every press/launch asset ([51](../09-gtm/51-launch-strategy.md))                                               | Launch narrative, press kit ([65](../12-corporate/65-press-kit.md))                                                    |
| Post-launch           | Analyst briefings with rubric; invite scoring of all vendors                                                                        | AR deck ([66](../12-corporate/66-analyst-relations.md))                                                                |
| Scale                 | Community + events own the vocabulary ([62](../11-marketing/62-community-strategy.md), [63](../11-marketing/63-events-strategy.md)) | Category report, annual survey                                                                                         |

**Contested-category playbook:** when a giant adopts similar language (expected — Microsoft/Salesforce will say "humans and agents working together"), we respond with the rubric, not volume: publish the five requirements, grade publicly, and emphasize the two they will fail — _owned truth_ (their truth is their silo) and _mandatory approval_ (their pitch is autonomy). Their adoption of our language is distribution for the category; the rubric keeps the definition ours.

**KPIs:** category-term search volume and share of voice ([55 SEO](../10-website/55-seo-strategy.md)); analyst usage of our definition; % of inbound describing us in category terms; competitor adoption of the vocabulary (a _win_ indicator, tracked not feared).
