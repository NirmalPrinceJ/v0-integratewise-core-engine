# 15 — Elevator Pitch

Status: CANONICAL · Commercial OS
Owner: Product Marketing
Last verified: 2026-07-21

---

## Purpose

The pitch at every length — 10 seconds to 2 minutes — for every audience, memorizable and consistent. Everyone at IntegrateWise delivers these verbatim-or-better.

**Target audience:** Everyone who ever answers "so what does IntegrateWise do?"

---

## 1. Ten Seconds (the hook)

> "IntegrateWise is the workspace where you and an AI Twin do the work together — one shared workbench instead of thirty tabs, and nothing happens without your approval."

Alternative (problem-first):

> "You know how you're the one copy-pasting between all your tools, and every AI you try forgets everything? We fixed both — in one workspace."

## 2. Thirty Seconds (the standard)

> "Your tools don't talk to each other, and your AI doesn't know your context — so _you_ end up being the bridge: copy-pasting between systems, re-explaining everything to a chatbot that forgets by tomorrow.
> IntegrateWise is the workspace for human and AI collaborative work. Your tools connect into a spine of truth your organization owns. You work on a shared workbench with your Twin — it already knows the context, prepares the hard parts, and proposes next steps with evidence. You decide. Nothing executes without your approval, and everything leaves an audit trail.
> AI that remembers what you told it, finishes what it started, and doesn't make things up."

## 3. Two Minutes (the narrative — meetings, podcasts, intros)

**[Problem — 30s]** "Every business runs on dozens of tools, and nobody has one view. A customer success manager opens eight tabs to understand a single account. And here's the thing AI was supposed to fix — instead it made it worse. Copilots got bolted onto each silo. Chatbots start from zero every session. Their memory auto-writes itself — it guesses. So people did what you'd expect: they quietly demoted AI to writing emails. The real work still runs on copy-paste, and the person is still the integration layer.

**[Product — 45s]** IntegrateWise recenters all of it. It's a workspace — your tools stay, but the work moves to one place. Underneath is the Adaptive Spine: your organization's operational truth, assembled from every connected system, with a source on every fact. On top are shared workbenches, composed for each role — accounts for CS, pipeline for sales. And here's the part that's different: you don't work _near_ the AI, you work _with_ it. Every person has a Twin on their bench. It knows the context because it shares your workbench. It notices what changed, prepares your brief, drafts the follow-up, proposes the next step — with evidence attached. You approve before anything real happens. Your organization's memory only learns what you verify.

**[Why us / why now — 30s]** I was a MuleSoft architect and a CSM — I lived both halves of this problem, and I built the spine that saved an $8M account before I built it as a product. The timing is right because the trust crisis is real: most enterprises now _require_ human oversight of AI. Everyone else is racing toward autonomy. We built the opposite bet: AI never owns the truth, humans approve everything — and that's exactly why teams finally let AI do consequential work.

**[Close — 10s]** Truth you own. AI you rent. Approval in between. That's IntegrateWise."

## 4. Audience Variants (swap the middle, keep the frame)

| Audience     | Emphasis swap                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Investor     | Add: "Copilots defend silos; assistants own memory. The neutral workspace over shared, governed state is the layer with the switching costs — and nobody credible owns it." ([67](../12-corporate/67-investor-narrative.md)) |
| CS leader    | Add: "Design partners cut QBR prep from 4 hours to 12 minutes. Churn signals surface before the health score moves."                                                                                                         |
| Security/IT  | Add: "Approval-gated memory, per-field provenance, tenant isolation at the database, full audit. The AI structurally cannot write truth or act alone."                                                                       |
| Practitioner | Drop architecture entirely; tell the 8:55am morning story: "Workbench opens on what changed, what needs you, what's prepared."                                                                                               |

## 5. Delivery Rules

- Never open with "we're an AI company." Open with the person's pain or the category.
- One vocabulary word maximum per pitch for practitioners (Twin). Executives get two (Twin, Spine).
- End every pitch with a question, not a feature: _"What's your team's version of the eight-tab morning?"_
- The forbidden words apply ([18 §6](../03-positioning-messaging/18-terminology-guide.md)): no "platform," "copilot," "assistant," "dashboard."

**KPIs:** pitch consistency (any teammate, any time — spot-checked); listener restatement accuracy; meetings booked per 10 pitches at events ([63](../11-marketing/63-events-strategy.md)).
