## Purpose

The mission is the vision made operational: what we do, for whom, every day. Where the vision describes the destination, the mission is the engine. It must fit in one breath and survive ten years.

**Target audience:** Team (primary), customers, candidates, press.

---

## The Mission

> **Give every person a workspace where they and their Twin do the work together — with context that persists, actions that are governed, and truth their organization owns.**

Short form (spoken): _"We're ending the era where people are the integration layer between their own tools — and giving AI a place to earn trust instead of demanding it."_

---

## Decomposition — What the Mission Obligates

| Mission clause                                               | Daily obligation                                                                                                                          | Owned by              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| "a workspace where they and their Twin do the work together" | Ship shared workbenches where interventions land inside the work, never a chat destination beside it (product doctrine, interaction laws) | Product               |
| "context that persists"                                      | Kill the Empty Context Tax: work resumes where it ended, across sessions, tools, people, and models                                       | Platform              |
| "actions that are governed"                                  | The Twin prepares; the human approves; everything audits. No exceptions, including for us                                                 | Platform + Governance |
| "truth their organization owns"                              | Spine is exportable, provider-agnostic, provenance-carrying. Leaving must be possible for staying to be meaningful                        | Platform              |

---

## Mission Boundaries

**We do:** compose work context, prepare hard parts, propose grounded actions, execute approved capabilities across connected tools, preserve organizational continuity.

**We don't:** replace the customer's tools of record, own their execution stack decisions, automate away the accountable human, or let any model — including the best one — write organizational truth directly.

---

## The Mission Test (for every quarter's plan)

1. Did we reduce the minutes a user spends reconstructing context? (measured: time-to-context on workbench open)
2. Did we increase the consequential work users trust the Twin to prepare? (measured: Approved Twin Actions per seat per week)
3. Did we strengthen the customer's ownership of their truth? (measured: Spine export capability, provider-swap capability, provenance coverage)

A quarter that ships features but moves none of these three failed the mission regardless of the changelog. ([76 Quarterly OKRs](../13-operations/76-quarterly-okrs.md) inherits these as standing objectives.)

**KPIs:** the three mission metrics above, plus employee mission-recall (every teammate can state the mission verbatim — checked in onboarding, [71 Internal Enablement](../13-operations/71-internal-enablement.md)).
