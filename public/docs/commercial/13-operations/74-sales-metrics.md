## Purpose

The sales measurement system: stage instrumentation, the weekly pipeline discipline, and the honest funnel math that tells us whether the motion is repeatable — the question that gates hiring and fundraising ([36 §4](../07-sales/36-sales-methodology.md), [50 §2](../09-gtm/50-gtm-strategy.md)).

**Target audience:** Founder-seller; AE #1; investors (diligence view).

---

## 1. Stage Instrumentation (CRM mirrors [36 §2] exactly)

Every stage transition logs its exit-criteria facts — a deal moves when facts exist, not when optimism does:

| Transition | Target | Red flag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S0 → S1 (ICP ≥ 9 → discovery held) | ≥ 60% of pursued | Low: targeting problem ([33](../06-customers/33-ideal-customer-profile.md)) |
| S1 → S2 (discovery → demo attended) | ≥ 60% | Low: discovery outputs weak ([38 KPIs](../07-sales/38-discovery-guide.md)) |
| S2 → S3 (demo → POV signed) | ≥ 40–50% | Low: demo or POV-offer problem ([39](../07-sales/39-demo-guide.md)) |
| S3 → S4 (POV → commit) | ≥ 60% | Low: success criteria or activation problem — check with [75](75-customer-success-metrics.md) |
| S4 → Won (commit → signature) | ≥ 80% | Low: procurement friction ([43](../07-sales/43-procurement-faq.md)) or economic-buyer gap |
| **S1 → Won (end-to-end)** | **≥ 20%** | The repeatability headline |

**Velocity:** S1→Won ≤ 90 days · POV never > 30 days without written reason ([37 Play 4](../07-sales/37-sales-playbook.md)) · S4 ≤ 3 weeks · any deal at 2× stage-typical duration gets a downgrade-or-revive decision.

## 2. The Weekly Pipeline Review (Monday, 30 min — the founder's sales hygiene [37](../07-sales/37-sales-playbook.md))

Fixed agenda: (1) coverage vs. ≥ 3x target · (2) stage-aging exceptions with decisions · (3) MEDDICC gaps on S3+ deals (two red letters at S3 = no Commit entry [36 §3](../07-sales/36-sales-methodology.md)) · (4) single-threaded S2+ deals flagged ([34 choreography](../06-customers/34-buyer-personas.md)) · (5) this week's three most-important deal actions, named.

**Hygiene rules:** every deal carries ICP score, personas engaged, competitor + card used, source (content/outbound/network/event — feeds [73]/[63] attribution) · loss reasons coded (category-misread / competitor-[name] / no-decision / price / security / timing) within a week of loss · win/loss notes feed the Friday ritual and monthly readout ([71 §3](71-internal-enablement.md)).

## 3. The Funnel Math (the model the metrics must prove)

Working backward from [77 plan](77-annual-gtm-plan.md) 2027 targets (~25 new customers in the year):

```
25 wins ÷ 20% S1→Won  = 125 discoveries  ≈ 2.4/week
125 discoveries ÷ 60% booking  ≈ 210 qualified conversations
   ← sourced: content 60% ([73]) + outbound/network/events 40%
Capacity check: 2.4 discoveries + ~1.6 demos + POV load ≈ founder's 15 selling hrs/wk — tight but real.
   The math says: protect selling hours ruthlessly ([50 §5]) and hire AE #1 on plan, not on hope.
```

This block is the repeatability dashboard: when actuals hold these ratios for two consecutive quarters, the motion is provably repeatable — AE #1 hires against it ([36 §4](../07-sales/36-sales-methodology.md)).

## 4. Founder-Sales Specific Metrics (pre-AE)

Selling-hours actual vs. 15/wk plan (monthly audit — the leakage metric) · recap-email same-day rate 100% · discovery 8-outputs completion 100% ([38](../07-sales/38-discovery-guide.md)) · demo checklist compliance 100% ([39 §3](../07-sales/39-demo-guide.md)) · POV weekly-review completion 100%. These process metrics *are* the early sales metrics: at n<20 deals, conversion percentages are noise; process compliance is signal.

**KPIs (the scoreboard):** S1→Won ≥ 20% · POV win ≥ 60% · cycle ≤ 90 days · coverage ≥ 3x · multi-threaded ≥ 80% · loss-reason coding 100% · repeatability ratios held 2 consecutive quarters (the gate).
