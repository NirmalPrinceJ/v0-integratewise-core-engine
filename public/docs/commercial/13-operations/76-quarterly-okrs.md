## Purpose

The quarterly operating contract: 3 objectives maximum, key results that are numbers, and the standing mission metrics that never rotate ([03 §the-mission-test](../01-foundation/03-mission.md)). Plus the current quarter's actual OKRs — this file always holds the live set.

**Target audience:** Founder (self-contract); future team (the ritual they inherit); investors (operating discipline evidence).

---

## 1. The OKR Rules (sized for reality)

Max 3 objectives, max 3 KRs each — a solo founder with 9 numbers is focused; with 20, decorated · KRs are outcomes, not activities ("4 paying customers" ✓, "run 40 demos" ✗ — activities live in the playbooks) · every KR maps to a phase-gate ([50 §2](../09-gtm/50-gtm-strategy.md)) or a mission metric · scoring: 0.0–1.0, target 0.7 (all 1.0s = sandbagged; all 0.3s = fantasy) · misses get one honest sentence of why, logged — the misses ledger is strategy input, not shame.

**Standing mission metrics (reported every quarter, never rotated out — [03](../01-foundation/03-mission.md)):** time-to-context on workbench open · ATA/seat/wk · truth-ownership proofs (export capability, provider-swap capability, provenance coverage).

## 4. The Quarterly Ritual (half-day, protected)

1. Score last quarter (with the misses ledger) → 2. SWOT refresh ([10]) → 3. Phase-gate check ([50 §2]) — *gates override ambition: no next-phase OKRs while current-phase gates are open* → 4. Draft next quarter (3×3 max) → 5. Sanity: does every KR trace to the mission test? → 6. Publish internally; update this file.

**KPIs (of the system):** OKRs set before quarter starts 100% · scored within week 1 100% · average score 0.6–0.8 band · misses-ledger entries with strategy consequences (a miss that changed nothing was never a real KR).
