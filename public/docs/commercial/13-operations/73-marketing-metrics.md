## Purpose

What marketing measures, on the funnel our motion actually has (founder-led content → audience → conversations → pipeline), with the anti-vanity discipline our stage demands.

**Target audience:** PMM hat, founder, future marketing hire.

---

## 1. The Funnel (as it really is)

```
REACH        essay/post impressions · site visitors
AUDIENCE     newsletter subscribers · LinkedIn followers (ICP-share sampled)
ENGAGEMENT   replies · comments · reply-rate ([60 §3] the resonance metric)
CONVERSATION content-sourced discovery calls ([57 §3] DM protocol) · demo requests · POV applications
PIPELINE     content-attributed qualified opportunities (first-touch, honestly logged)
REVENUE      content-attributed closed-won (reported with attribution caveats, never overclaimed)
```

**Attribution posture (stage-honest):** first-touch + self-reported ("how did you hear about us?" is mandatory in every demo request form and discovery call — [38 §4](../07-sales/38-discovery-guide.md)). No multi-touch modeling theater until volume justifies it; a founder can *know* where 40 deals came from.

## 3. Message-Health Metrics (the PMM-specific set — measuring whether the *story* works)

Message pull-through: % of discovery calls where the prospect uses category/pillar language unprompted (call-recording sample, monthly) · misreading rate: "chatbot/dashboard/integration-tool" descriptions in inbound (trending to zero = positioning landing [11 §6](../03-positioning-messaging/11-positioning.md)) · voice-audit score ≥ 95% ([20 §4](../04-product-marketing/20-product-marketing-guide.md)) · claims-register freshness ≤ 90 days ([79](../14-appendix/79-appendix.md)).

## 4. Review Rituals

Weekly (15 min, inside the Friday ritual): conversation-layer numbers only — calls sourced, demo requests, what content drove them. Monthly (1 hr): full scorecard; kill/double decisions on formats ([56 §5](../11-marketing/56-content-strategy.md)); LP conversion review vs. [54] targets. Quarterly: positioning tests ([11 §6](../03-positioning-messaging/11-positioning.md)); SEO ring progress; the "which essays became pipeline" retro that tunes next quarter's calendar.

**Anti-vanity rules:** impressions never appear without their conversion neighbor · follower counts reported only with ICP-share · no metric that can't change a decision gets dashboard space.

**KPIs (of the function):** scorecard shipped monthly 100% · attribution field completeness ≥ 90% of new opportunities · decisions logged from reviews (a review without a decision was a meeting).
