## Purpose

The international plan with its stage-honest core: **we go deep before we go wide.** This document keeps the multi-market designs alive, defines the activation gates precisely, and prevents the two failure modes: premature expansion (splitting a solo founder across three GTMs) and expansion amnesia (rediscovering at Series A what was already designed).

**Target audience:** Founder; investors (TAM expansion narrative); future international hires.

---

## 1. The Sequencing Decision (and why)

The Tier A canon designed three simultaneous markets. The Commercial OS supersedes that with sequence ([50 §4](../09-gtm/50-gtm-strategy.md)): **English-first enterprise CS wedge (NA/UK/EU) → India SMB → UAE/GCC.** Three reasons: a solo founder cannot run three GTM motions (SWOT W2/W5 [10]); the enterprise wedge produces the proof assets (case studies, NRR evidence) that make volume markets cheaper to win later; and per-seat regional pricing ([30 §2]) plus the multi-market distribution designs mean activation is a *decision*, not a rebuild.

**What stays warm meanwhile (≤ 2% of founder time):** CA-network and free-zone relationships maintained ([48 P4](../09-gtm/48-partner-strategy.md)) · regional price lists published-ready · WhatsApp/Tally/Razorpay + Zoho connector designs preserved in the roadmap's *later* column ([69 §1](../12-corporate/69-product-roadmap-story.md)).

## 2. Market Dossiers (the designed assets, kept current)

**India SMB (63M+ MSMEs — the volume market):**
Wedge: client management for CAs and service businesses ("Account Success") · first connectors: WhatsApp + Tally + Razorpay · pricing: ₹499/₹1,499/₹2,999 per seat ([30 §2]) · distribution: CA networks, WhatsApp groups, Hindi YouTube — relationship-led, referral-priced (CAC $100–300 per canon) · key message: "Check WhatsApp + Tally + Razorpay + Sheets every morning? One workspace replaces all four." · localization needs: Hindi UI + WhatsApp-native workflows + GST/compliance entity types.

**UAE / GCC (200K+ FZEs — the margin market):**
Wedge: business ops for founder-operated FZEs (compliance, vendors, financial visibility) · first connectors: WhatsApp + Zoho Books + Stripe · pricing: $12/$39/$79 per seat · distribution: free-zone authorities, coworking ecosystems, LinkedIn · key message: "Trade license expiring. VAT filing due. Vendor contract renewing. One place sees it all." · needs: VAT/compliance signals, Arabic-optional UI.

**EU deepening (not a new motion — the same wedge, more geography):** gate: SOC 2 milestones + data-residency roadmap item shipping ([42 §2–3](../07-sales/42-security-faq.md)); then UK/DACH/Nordics prospecting inside the existing ICP ([33]).

## 3. Activation Gates (all three per market, no exceptions)

1. **Motion gate:** enterprise wedge at P2 Compound ([50 §2]) — repeatable without founder heroics (AE + CS hired and performing).
2. **Capability gate:** the market's wedge connectors production-ready + localization minimums shipped.
3. **Capacity gate:** a named owner for the market (regional hire or committed partner per [48 P4]) — a market without an owner is a distraction with a flag.

**Expected activation window (planning, not promise):** India scouting late 2027, activation 2028 post-Series A; UAE follows India by ~2 quarters (shared WhatsApp-first patterns); EU deepening continuous as certifications land.

## 4. What International Means for the Core (design consequences now)

Multi-currency billing architecture preserved in pricing/licensing design ([32]) · entity-type extensibility (GST invoices, VAT filings, trade licenses) stays a Spine design principle — the adaptive schema *is* the international product strategy · the category claim is global from day one: "Workspace for Human and AI Collaborative Work" translates; the manifesto ships in English, the vocabulary is coined to travel ([05](../02-category-market/05-category-definition.md)).

**KPIs:** gate status reviewed quarterly (a one-line health check in the OKR ritual [76 §4]) · warm-relationship maintenance (CA/free-zone contacts touched ≥ 2×/year) · zero premature international spend (the discipline metric) · at activation: market-specific funnel stands up within one quarter using this dossier.
