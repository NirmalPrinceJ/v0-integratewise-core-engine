## Purpose

The CS measurement system: adoption, outcome, and continuity metrics per account and portfolio-wide — the numbers that predict renewal a year before the renewal date.

**Target audience:** CS operator; founder; board (retention view).

---

## 1. The Three-Layer Account Scorecard (mirrors the health framework [44 §2](../08-customer-success/44-customer-success-playbook.md))

**Layer 1 — Adoption (is the product lived-in?):**

| Metric | Definition | Healthy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seat activation | Per [45 §3]: ≥5 approved actions + ≥4 workbench-first mornings/wk | ≥ 70% of seats by day 30 |
| ATA/seat/wk | North star, account-level ([72 §1]) | ≥ 5 floor · ≥ 8 mature |
| Workbench-first mornings | % of seat-days opening the bench before other tools | ≥ 60% |
| Intervention acceptance | Twin interventions acted on (accepted/edited) ÷ surfaced | ≥ 40% (below = noise-tuning play [45 wk2](../08-customer-success/45-onboarding-playbook.md)) |
| TruthLayer throughput | Memory proposals reviewed/wk (approve *or* reject — reviewing is the health signal) | ≥ 5/account/wk |

**Layer 2 — Outcomes (is it paying?):**

| Metric | Definition | Healthy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline completeness | Committed outcomes with instrumented baselines ([28 §4]) | 100% (structural — no baseline, no account) |
| Realized-vs-promised | Attained ÷ committed outcome values at review | ≥ 0.8 |
| Documented catches | Signals confirmed real + acted, with evidence chains | ≥ 1/account/quarter |
| Time reclaimed | Morning-minutes + prep-hours delta vs. baseline (sampled) | ≥ 60% of gathering time by day 90 |

**Layer 3 — Continuity & Relationship (will it compound?):**

| Metric | Definition | Healthy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spine knowledge volume | Approved memory entries + entity coverage growth | Growing monthly |
| Expansion triggers logged | Forwarded-morning / QBR-guest / handover-proof events ([46 §2]) | ≥ 1/account/quarter (leading NRR) |
| Sponsor engagement | P2 attendance at day-30/90/QBR cadence | 100% of scheduled |
| Review cadence health | Reviews held on schedule | 100%; 2 deferrals = play trigger ([44 §4]) |

## 2. Portfolio Metrics (roll-ups the board sees)

Activation rate across live onboardings · portfolio ATA/seat/wk trend · GRR ≥ 92% / NRR ≥ 120% (definitions [72 §2]; design-partner cohort separate) · renewal classification coverage: 100% G/Y/R by T−120 ([47 §5]) · catches portfolio-wide (the proof engine feeding [64](../12-corporate/64-case-study-templates.md)) · time-to-value: kickoff → first approved action ≤ 48h median ([45 KPIs]).

## 3. The Leading-Indicator Chain (what predicts what — manage upstream)

```
Day-2 first-morning quality → day-30 activation → day-60 ATA level →
day-90 realized-value ≥ 0.8 → expansion triggers → T−120 Green → renewal + NRR
```

Rule: intervene at the *leftmost* broken link ([44 play library]); a renewal saved at T−60 was actually saved at day 2. CS reviews report against this chain, not against a single health score — we sell against opaque scores; we don't manage by one ([09 Card 4](../02-category-market/09-competitive-battle-cards.md)).

## 4. Measurement Integrity

Outcomes counted from user-visible artifacts, never telemetry alone ([28 §4] anti-gaming) · time samples collected the same way at baseline and at review (like-for-like) · catches require the *customer's* confirmation that the signal was real · realized-vs-promised reported honestly at ≥ 0.8 target — an inflated 1.2 today is a churned account next year.

**KPIs (of the CS function):** portfolio activation ≥ 70% · realized-vs-promised ≥ 0.8 portfolio median · GRR/NRR at targets · review-cadence completion 100% · churn post-mortem completion 100% ([47 §5]) · case-study pipeline conversion ≥ 30% of day-90 reviews ([64 KPIs]).
