## 2. The Quarterly Motion Detail

**Q3 2026 — Prove.** All energy on the Strategic Build Plan's moment: real CSMs, real HubSpot data, lighter days. Sales activity: 15+ discoveries (network + early content), 2 conversions. No launch prep beyond the gate checklist's long-lead items (film, listing paperwork).

**Q4 2026 — Claim.** Launch when the gate passes, not when the calendar insists ([51 §1]). The raise runs on launch momentum. Pipeline target entering 2027: ≥ $150K qualified (3x Q1 target [72 §2]).

**Q1–Q2 2027 — Repeat.** The founder runs the machine while proving someone else could: every deal on-methodology, ratios tracked for the two-quarter repeatability test ([74 §3]), enablement corpus ready ([71]). AE #1 starts with a warm pipeline, certified in 2 weeks, ramp target: first close ≤ 60 days.

**Q3–Q4 2027 — Compound.** Expansion engine carries growing share (V2/V3 plays live [46]); partner Phase 2 activates on its gate ([48 §1]); the Series A narrative assembles from cohort evidence ([67 §8], NRR proof).
