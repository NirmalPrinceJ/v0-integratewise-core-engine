# 71 — Internal Enablement

Status: CANONICAL · Commercial OS
Owner: Founder → first hires' managers
Last verified: 2026-07-21

---

## Purpose

How anyone who joins IntegrateWise — employee, contractor, agency, advisor — learns to speak, sell, and operate the company correctly, fast. The Commercial OS is the curriculum; this document is the course plan and the certification bar.

**Target audience:** New hires (all roles), founder (as trainer), external partners who represent us.

---

## 1. The Enablement Principle

Enablement here is not a deck — it's **reading the operating system in the right order, then proving it.** Every commercial answer a new person needs already exists in these 79 documents; the job is sequencing and certification, not re-teaching.
