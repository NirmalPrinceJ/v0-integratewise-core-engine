## Purpose

Which partnerships matter, in what order, and the discipline that keeps partnerships from becoming a distraction. Stage truth: partnerships amplify a working motion; they never substitute for one. Until the direct wedge is repeatable, partner work is _seeding_, not _scaling_.

**Target audience:** Founder; future partnerships hire; investors (channel narrative).

---

## 1. Partner Thesis & Sequencing

| Phase                                  | Gate                                                                    | Partner focus                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Now (pre-repeatable)**               | —                                                                       | Seed only: relationships, one lighthouse services partner, marketplace listings prep ([49](49-marketplace-strategy.md))              |

## 2. The Four Partner Types (in priority order)

### P1 — MuleSoft / Salesforce Ecosystem (the unfair advantage)

**Why:** founder's home turf — architect + CSM credibility, existing network, Dreamforce presence ([63](../11-marketing/63-events-strategy.md)). SIs and architects in this ecosystem _are_ our ICP's trusted advisors, and they live the integration-without-context pain professionally.
**The play:** position IntegrateWise as what SIs recommend _after_ the integration project — "you connected the systems; now connect the context." ArchitectIQ angle (integration-landscape intelligence) gives them a diagnostic tool that opens their own client conversations.
**Economics (Phase 2):** referral 15% year-1 ARR · resell later, only with proven co-delivery.
**Now:** 5 architect/SI relationships warm; 1 co-authored piece ("integration health IS account health"); no formal program paper yet.

### P2 — CS Consultancies & Fractional CS Leaders

**Why:** they design CS processes for exactly our ICP; a consultant recommendation converts at champion-strength. Our product makes _their_ deliverable (a working CS operating model) demonstrably better.
**The play:** certified-partner-lite — free internal use (their own client books run on us: Customer-Zero-by-proxy) + referral fee + co-marketing (webinars [61](../11-marketing/61-webinar-strategy.md)).
**Now:** 3 relationships; 1 joint webinar per quarter target.

### P3 — Execution-Stack Vendors (automation/agent runtimes)

**Why:** the handoff architecture makes n8n/Zapier/agent-runtime users natural adjacencies — we're the governed judgment in front of their hands ([26](../04-product-marketing/26-capability-fabric-story.md)). Their communities contain ops-minded buyers who already believe in tooling.
**The play:** technical integration content ("governed handoff to [X]") + directory/marketplace listings + community presence. Never co-sell dependence — they're feature-adjacent, not channel.

### P4 — Regional Distribution (deferred with [78](../13-operations/78-international-expansion.md))

CA networks (India) and free-zone authorities/coworking ecosystems (UAE) — the canon's multi-market channels, preserved and explicitly parked until regional activation gates are met. No investment now beyond keeping the relationships alive.

## 3. Partner Discipline (the anti-distraction rules)

1. **Partner time-box (pre-Phase 2): ≤ 10% of founder GTM time.** Every partner hour is a direct-selling hour spent; the wedge pays for everything.
2. **No partner-led product commitments.** Partner feature requests enter the same roadmap triage as everything else ([69](../12-corporate/69-product-roadmap-story.md)); no bespoke builds for a promised channel.
3. **Prove-then-program:** a partner type gets formal paper (agreements, margins, portal) only after 2 organic wins from that type. Programs formalize what's working; they don't conjure it.
4. **Partner-sourced deals run our methodology** ([36](../07-sales/36-sales-methodology.md)) — same qualification, same POV, same paper. A partner intro is a warmer S0, not a different sales process.
5. **Channel conflict pre-empted:** direct-named accounts list maintained from Phase 2; referral registration first-in-first-credited; disputes resolved for the customer's experience, always.

**KPIs:** Phase-gates hit before program spend · partner-sourced pipeline (tracked from now, expected ≈ 0 pre-Phase 2 — honesty in the dashboard) · Phase 2: partner-sourced ≥ 15% of pipeline; partner deal win-rate ≥ direct (quality bar); time-box compliance.
