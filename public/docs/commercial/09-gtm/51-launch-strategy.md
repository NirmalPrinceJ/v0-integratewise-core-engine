## Purpose

The public launch: what it must accomplish, what must be true first, the launch architecture, and the two-week run-of-show. A launch is a claim staked in public — we stake it once, with proof behind it.

**Target audience:** Founder; PMM hat ([20 §3](../04-product-marketing/20-product-marketing-guide.md) tiering: this is the T1 narrative launch).

---

## 1. Launch Thesis

We do not launch a product; we launch a **category with a product as its proof**. The launch's one job: make "Workspace for Human and AI Collaborative Work" a phrase the market has now heard, attached to a founder story it remembers, and a demo it can verify. Signups are the byproduct; the category claim is the asset.

**Timing gate (all must be true — target window: Q4 2026, after P0 exit criteria [50 §2](50-gtm-strategy.md)):**

- [ ] 4+ paying customers with activation ≥ 70%
- [ ] 2+ design partners with quotable, numbered outcomes (named or "design partner" attributed)
- [ ] The end-to-end flow solid for self-serve Starter/Growth (launch traffic must land on a working product)
- [ ] Demo environment + backup recording current ([39 §5](../07-sales/39-demo-guide.md))
- [ ] Security packet public-ready ([42](../07-sales/42-security-faq.md))
- [ ] Website live per [53 Website Copy](../10-website/53-website-copy.md); HubSpot marketplace listing live ([49](49-marketplace-strategy.md))

Launching before the gate = launching a promise. Launching after = launching evidence. We do the second.

## 2. Launch Architecture (three layers, one week apart)

**Layer 1 — The Manifesto (D−7):** the category essay, founder-bylined: the four-act narrative ([04](../01-foundation/04-long-term-narrative.md)) compressed to 1,500 words, ending in the category definition and rubric ([05 §3](../02-category-market/05-category-definition.md)). Published on the site ([54 category page](../10-website/54-landing-pages.md)), syndicated on LinkedIn ([59](../11-marketing/59-linkedin-strategy.md)), sent to the newsletter list ([60](../11-marketing/60-newsletter-strategy.md)). The manifesto primes the vocabulary so launch-day coverage uses our words.

**Layer 2 — The Product (D-day):** "IntegrateWise is generally available." Assets: launch post (the Day narrative [19 §2](../04-product-marketing/19-product-narrative.md) with real screenshots) · 3-minute product film following Maya's morning (the launch-film spine: AUTHORIZE → CONNECT → RETAIN → CONTINUE → ACT → REMEMBER, canon) · design-partner proof quotes · pricing public ([30](../05-value-pricing/30-pricing-strategy.md)) · Product Hunt + Hacker News (founder-answered comments all day — the comment thread *is* the launch event) · press outreach per [65 Press Kit](../12-corporate/65-press-kit.md).

**Layer 3 — The Proof (D+7):** the first named case study ([64](../12-corporate/64-case-study-templates.md)) + "what we learned from 8 design partners" essay — honest numbers, including what didn't work (the honesty *is* the differentiation; it also pre-empts the "too good to be true" reflex the demoted-AI audience carries).

## 3. Run of Show (D-day)

| Time (ET) | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 00:01 | Product Hunt live; site hero switches to launch state |
| 06:00 | Launch post live; newsletter sends; LinkedIn founder post #1 (the story) |
| 08:00 | HN Show post; personal notes to the 50 warmest network contacts (pre-drafted, individually edited) |
| 09:00–18:00 | Founder answers every comment, every channel; team-of-one war room discipline: 30-min triage cycles |
| 12:00 | LinkedIn post #2 (the demo film) |
| 18:00 | Metrics snapshot; thank-you post; day-1 retro notes |
| D+1 → D+5 | One follow-up asset daily: governance deep-dive, Spine story, pricing rationale, demotion-story essay, design-partner learnings |

## 4. Launch Metrics (48h / 30d)

| Metric | 48h target | 30d target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unique visitors | 10,000 | 25,000 |
| Demo requests / POV applications | 40 | 150 |
| Starter/Growth signups | 100 | 400 |
| Signup → activated seat | — | ≥ 25% |
| Category-phrase pickup (coverage/posts using our words) | 5 instances | 20 instances |
| Newsletter net adds | 500 | 1,500 |
| Sales-qualified conversations | 10 | 40 |

**Honest expectation-setting:** launch spikes decay ~90% by week 2. The plan above treats launch as a *permission event* — the moment the market grants attention — feeding the durable engines ([56 Content](../11-marketing/56-content-strategy.md), [60 Newsletter](../11-marketing/60-newsletter-strategy.md)). Post-launch, the weekly rhythm resumes as if launch never happened; that discipline is what separates a launch from a sugar high.

## 5. Risk Table

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Traffic breaks self-serve onboarding | Load-test before gate; Starter flow rehearsed; waitlist valve ready |
| "Another AI tool" dismissal in comments | Founder answers with the refusal demo clip + governance receipts, never adjectives |
| Giant announces adjacent language near our window | Ship anyway; the manifesto + rubric is the counter ([05 §5](../02-category-market/05-category-definition.md)); their noise is our category's distribution |
| Demo-day incident | Backup film; status-page honesty; incident template ([65](../12-corporate/65-press-kit.md)) |

**KPIs:** gate completion before date-setting (binary); 48h/30d table above; category-phrase pickup (the launch's true north); % launch signups from ICP ([33](../06-customers/33-ideal-customer-profile.md)) — volume without fit is noise.
