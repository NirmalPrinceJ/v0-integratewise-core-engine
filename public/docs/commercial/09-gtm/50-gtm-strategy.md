## Purpose

The one-page-deep answer to "how does IntegrateWise get to market?" — motion, phases, channel architecture, and the resource allocation that makes a solo-founder GTM survivable. The annual numbers live in [77 Annual GTM Plan](../13-operations/77-annual-gtm-plan.md); this is the strategy they implement.

**Target audience:** Founder (operating), investors (diligence), first GTM hires (context).

---

## 1. The Motion (one sentence)

> **Founder-led, content-fed, proof-gated:** the founder sells directly into the CS wedge of B2B SaaS companies, fed by founder-led category content, with every deal passing through a 30-day proof-of-value on the customer's real stack — then expansion does the growing.

Why this motion (and not PLG-first or sales-team-first): the product's value is *organizational* (shared truth, governed collaboration) — it demos brilliantly but self-serves shallowly pre-activation, so pure PLG under-converts; and pre-repeatability, hired sellers burn cash learning what only the founder can currently articulate ([36 §4](../07-sales/36-sales-methodology.md) defines the repeatability tests). Starter/Growth self-serve exists as a *side door and future engine*, not the current strategy.

## 2. The Phases

| Phase | Period | Goal | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P0 Prove** | Now → Q4 2026 | The Strategic Build Plan's moment: real users, real data, lighter days. 8 design partners → 4 paying | 4 paying · activation ≥ 70% pattern · 1 documented catch/account · repeatable demo→POV→close |
| **P1 Repeat** | Q1–Q3 2027 | Same motion, held steady: 25 customers, the playbooks proven by someone other than their author | 25 customers · S1→Won ≥ 20% · POV win ≥ 60% · 2 named case studies · AE #1 hired against the repeatability tests |
| **P2 Compound** | Q4 2027 → | Expansion engine + channel seeding + launch amplification | NRR ≥ 120% cohort evidence · partner-sourced ≥ 15% pipeline · Series A metrics ([68](../12-corporate/68-fundraising-story.md)) |

**Phase discipline:** no P2 spend (partner programs, paid acquisition, second market) while P0/P1 exit criteria are open. The [SWOT's W5](../02-category-market/10-swot-analysis.md) — breadth temptation — is the standing risk; this table is its control.

## 4. The Wedge Logic (why CS, why HubSpot, why this order — canonized from the Strategic Build Plan)

CS teams feel the fragmentation pain **most measurably** (3 hrs/day, churn surprises with dollar signs) · CRM data is the **richest first hydration** (accounts, contacts, deals) · HubSpot's APIs and OAuth are the **fastest path to a real morning** · CSM workflows produce **provable, narratable outcomes** (QBR prep, catches) that convert into case studies and expansion pull. Sales/RevOps expansion inherits everything ([46 V3](../08-customer-success/46-expansion-strategy.md)). India/UAE volume markets: designed, priced ([30 §2](../05-value-pricing/30-pricing-strategy.md)), and **deferred** behind explicit gates ([78](../13-operations/78-international-expansion.md)).

## 5. GTM Resource Reality (solo founder + future hires)

**Founder week (GTM half, ~25 hrs):** selling 15 (calls, POVs, proposals) · content 4 (one essay + LinkedIn dailies batched) · CS 4 (design-partner cadences — also product discovery) · ops 2 (CRM hygiene, metrics Friday ritual).

**Hiring order (each gated, [77](../13-operations/77-annual-gtm-plan.md) budgets):** 1) founding AE (P1 exit) · 2) CS #1 (at 15 customers — before CS quality dilutes) · 3) content/PMM (when founder-content demand exceeds 4 hrs sustainably) · 4) SE (when technical-eval volume warrants). Marketing leadership waits for Series A.

**The math this must produce ([06 §2 SOM](../02-category-market/06-market-analysis.md)):** ~500 engaged accounts → 40 customers by end-2027 at ~$22K blended ACV ≈ $0.9M ARR, NRR compounding thereafter — consistent with FINANCIAL_MODEL trajectory, honest about the per-seat re-basing.

**KPIs:** phase exit-criteria attainment (the only GTM scoreboard that matters) · pipeline coverage ≥ 3x each quarter · CAC by channel (founder-led target < $1K blended) · founder-time allocation audit monthly (the plan above vs. reality).
