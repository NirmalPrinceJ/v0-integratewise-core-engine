# Positioning (canonical statement)

For teams whose work is scattered across dozens of disconnected tools — and whose AI experiments quietly failed — IntegrateWise is the workspace for human and AI collaborative work. Every person works alongside a governed Twin on a shared workbench, over operational truth the organization owns. Unlike copilots bolted onto single apps, automation tools that move data without understanding it, or assistants that forget everything, IntegrateWise gives humans and AI one place to do the work together — with memory that persists, actions that require approval, and truth that never belongs to the AI.

**The two forks nobody else takes** (every competitive conversation resolves here): owned truth above the silo · mandatory approval on memory and action. Silo vendors can't sell truth-above-the-silo; engagement- and autonomy-economics vendors can't gate themselves.

# Message hierarchy

L0 Category → L1 Promise ("This is what you wanted. How you wanted it to work.") → L2 Mechanism (Human + Twin, Shared Workbench, Adaptive Spine, Governance) → L3 Pillars (One Workspace · Twin on the Bench · Truth You Own) → L4 Proof. Enter at one level, descend only — promise without mechanism is hype; proof without promise is a spec sheet.

# By audience

- **CSM/AM:** "Your morning is eight tabs. It should be one." — lead with the morning, never with "AI."
- **VP CS:** "Churn signals before the score moves; QBR prep in minutes; your team's 3 hours a day back."
- **COO/Founder:** "Your company's context lives in tools and leaves with people. Make it organizational."
- **RevOps:** "One governed truth across your stack — without replacing anything. Provenance on every field."
- **CISO:** "AI that structurally cannot write truth or act unapproved — architecture, not policy."
- **Investor:** "Everyone bolts copilots onto silos. Nobody owns where humans and AI work together over shared, governed state."

# Long-form arc (fixed)

BEFORE (AI looked magical → forgot → drifted → wrong) → TODAY (the Quiet Demotion) → PROMISE → MECHANISM (Workspace · Twin · Spine · Governance) → OUTCOME (tell it once; it remembers; it follows through; it finishes). Open long-form with the Demotion Story. The human is always the hero; the Twin is the ally — never reversed.
