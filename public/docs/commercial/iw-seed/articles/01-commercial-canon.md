# The Canon

**Category:** Workspace for Human and AI Collaborative Work. Never position as: AI chatbot, assistant, copilot, integration platform, iPaaS, dashboard, knowledge base, or "operating system/platform" as a category label (DECISION 23).

**One-liner:** IntegrateWise is the workspace where humans and their Twins do the work together — one shared workbench, governed actions, and truth the organization owns.

**Plain form:** AI that remembers what you told it, finishes what it started, and doesn't make things up.

**Product stack:** Workspace → Shared Workbench → Human + Twin Collaboration → Capability Execution → Organizational Continuity.

## Canonical lines (quote exactly)

- "Truth you own. AI you rent. Approval in between." — brand doctrine
- "My work already knows its context. AI prepared the hard parts. I decide." — experience promise
- "Every AI remembers. Only IntegrateWise remembers the truth." — trust line
- "The Twin prepares. The human approves." — governance law
- "AI never owns organizational truth. The Adaptive Spine owns truth." — architecture doctrine
- "The next piece of work starts where the last one ended." — continuity promise
- "Dashboards show work. Workbenches do work." — vs. BI
- "Copilots visit your apps. IntegrateWise is where work lives." — vs. copilots

## The narrative frame

Three failures → three pillars: **Fragmentation** ("I'm the Human API") → **One Workspace** · **Amnesia** ("I told it once. It forgot.") → **A Twin on the Bench** · **Anarchy** ("It did something. Nobody approved it.") → **Truth You Own**.

**The Four Enemies:** Amnesia · Babysitting · Hallucination · Human API. **The Empty Context Tax:** auth again, connect again, explain again, search again, reconstruct again, start again. **The Quiet Demotion:** the ~80% who tried AI, got burned, and downgraded it to writing emails — our exact market; they need trust restored, not education.

## Core definitions (verbatim)

- **Workspace:** the single environment where an organization's people and their Twins do their work.
- **Shared Workbench:** a role-composed working surface, projected from the Adaptive Spine, where a human and their Twin collaborate on the same live work.
- **Twin:** a person's governed AI counterpart — prepares, suggests, proposes; never owns truth, never acts without approval.
- **Adaptive Spine:** the canonical, governed operational state of the organization — the system that owns truth.
- **TruthLayer:** the approval gate on organizational memory — AI proposes, human approves, only then knowledge enters the Spine.
- **Governed Action:** work performed under policy with approval and audit.

## Voice rules — never say

"Operating System"/"platform" as category · "Capability Fabric" in customer-facing copy (internal name only; say "governed actions across your connected tools") · "assistant/chatbot/copilot" for the Twin · "replace your CRM" · "AI that runs your business" / autonomy promises · competitor names in advertising.
