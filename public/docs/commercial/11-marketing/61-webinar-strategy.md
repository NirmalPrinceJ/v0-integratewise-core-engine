# 61 — Webinar Strategy

Status: CANONICAL · Commercial OS
Owner: Founder + PMM hat
Last verified: 2026-07-21

---

## Purpose

Live formats that convert warm audience into evaluators — run lean (one repeatable flagship, monthly) with every asset recycled from the content engine. Webinars are the middle of our funnel: after the essay, before the POV ([35 stage 2→3](../06-customers/35-customer-journey.md)).

**Target audience:** Founder (host), PMM hat (production), future marketing hire.

---

## 1. The Flagship: "The Eight-Tab Morning" (monthly, 45 min)

One webinar, perfected and repeated — not a webinar *program*:

| Segment | Min | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The ritual, itemized | 0–10 | The teardown ([56 F2](56-content-strategy.md)): a CSM morning, minute-by-minute, the tax computed live with audience numbers (poll: "how many tools?") |
| Why AI made it worse | 10–20 | The demotion arc; the Four Enemies; why copilots multiplied the silos ([12 §2](../03-positioning-messaging/12-messaging-framework.md)) |
| The morning, replaced | 20–35 | **Live product** — the Day demo ([40](../07-sales/40-demo-script.md) beats 2–5 compressed): workbench open → evidence → Ask/Assign/**Approve** → the refusal + audit trail |
| Q&A + the ask | 35–45 | Honest answers ([41](../07-sales/41-objection-handling.md) discipline); the POV offer ([54 LP-6](../10-website/54-landing-pages.md)); replay + newsletter for registrants |

**Why this shape:** the audience is the Quiet Demotion demographic — they need their skepticism *respected* (segments 1–2) before a demo earns attention (segment 3). The refusal beat stays in even at webinar scale; it converts skeptics better than any feature.

## 2. Production System (≤ 4 hours/month total)

Registration page from the LP template ([54 standards](../10-website/54-landing-pages.md)): one job, the promise, three bullets, form (name/email/company/CS team size — doubles as ICP qualification) · promotion: 2 LinkedIn posts + 1 newsletter PS + sales-sequence slots ([37](../07-sales/37-sales-playbook.md) nurture) · dry-run the demo environment same checklist as sales demos ([39 §3](../07-sales/39-demo-guide.md)) · replay to registrants within 24h; edited highlights (the approval moment, the refusal) become excerpt posts ([58 §2](58-social-media-strategy.md)).

**Partner variant (quarterly):** co-hosted with a CS consultancy ([48 P2](../09-gtm/48-partner-strategy.md)) — their audience, our demo, shared list per consent rules. Same deck, guest segment swapped in.

## 3. Follow-Up Doctrine (where webinar ROI actually lives)

Within 24h, three-way split of attendees: **ICP + engaged** (asked questions, poll answers ≥ 8 tools) → personal founder note referencing *their* question + POV invitation · **ICP + passive** → replay + the demotion essay + newsletter enrollment · **non-ICP** → replay + newsletter only. No generic "thanks for attending" blasts — every ICP touch is specific or skipped.

## 4. What We Don't Do

Panel-heavy thought-leader theater (no product, no pipeline) · gated-replay lead-gen spam (replays are open after 30 days; the list quality matters more than form-fills) · weekly cadence (the flagship monthly + partner quarterly is the founder-hour ceiling) · webinar-platform enterprise contracts pre-Series A.

**KPIs:** registrations/webinar (target 100 by launch quarter, 60% ICP) · attendance ≥ 40% of registrants · attendee → POV application ≥ 5% · attendee → newsletter ≥ 50% · demo-segment drop-off < 20% (if people leave during the demo, the demo has a problem — feed back to [39](../07-sales/39-demo-guide.md)).
