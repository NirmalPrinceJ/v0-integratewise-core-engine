## Purpose

The platform playbook for our single most important channel: where CS leaders, RevOps heads, and COOs — the buying committee ([34](../06-customers/34-buyer-personas.md)) — scroll professionally. Everything here executes the content engine ([56](56-content-strategy.md)) and founder-led doctrine ([57](57-founder-led-marketing.md)) on LinkedIn's specific physics.

**Target audience:** Founder (daily operator).

---

## 1. Positioning on the Platform

The founder's profile is a landing page — optimize it once, properly:

- **Headline:** `Building IntegrateWise — the workspace for human and AI collaborative work. Ex-MuleSoft architect & CSM. I was the Human API; now I'm retiring the job.`
- **About:** the 60-second founder story ([14 §2](../01-foundation/14-founder-story.md)) verbatim, ending with the doctrine line and a "see the morning" link.
- **Featured:** the manifesto ([54 LP-1](../10-website/54-landing-pages.md)) · the Day film · the POV page ([54 LP-6](../10-website/54-landing-pages.md)).
- **Experience entries** tell the two-sided credential (architect + CSM) as narrative, not job bullets.

## 2. The Post System (formats with LinkedIn physics)

**Weekly skeleton (from [58 §4](58-social-media-strategy.md)):** Mon long-post (the essay, natively rewritten) · Tue–Thu excerpts · Fri build-log.

**Format templates (complete, reusable):**

**F1 — The Wound Story (highest performer for our audience):**
> Line 1 (the hook, standalone): `An $8M account went red. Everyone had the data. Nobody had the picture.`
> Then: 8–12 short lines telling the story ([56 formats](56-content-strategy.md)) · one structural lesson · no CTA (credibility posts sell by not selling). Product mention: zero.

**F2 — The Teardown:**
> Hook: `I counted every click of a CSM's morning. 8 tools. 47 minutes. Before the first customer call.`
> Body: the itemized ritual → the math (H × C × N napkin) → "this is a tax, not a job." CTA-light: "I write about ending this weekly → [newsletter]."

**F3 — The Demotion Mirror:**
> Hook: `You didn't stop believing in AI. You stopped believing in *that* AI.`
> Body: the demotion story ([12 §3](../03-positioning-messaging/12-messaging-framework.md)) → the three failures (Amnesia, Babysitting, Hallucination) → the principle (approval restores trust). Soft bridge allowed in final line.

**F4 — The Build Log (T4):**
> Hook: `Our signal engine was too noisy for three versions. Design partners told us. Here's v4's numbers.`
> Body: honest metrics, what changed, screenshot evidence. These posts convert *skeptics* — our exact market.

**F5 — The Contrarian Take (used sparingly, 2×/month max):**
> Hook: `Autonomous agents are a governance incident with a roadmap.`
> Body: the argument with mechanisms, not adjectives → our counter-principle ("The Twin prepares. The human approves."). Expect argument in comments; that's the distribution.

**Mechanics:** first line must survive truncation (it's the whole ad) · white space generously · links in first comment · 0–1 hashtags (they're dead weight) · post 8–9am ET Tue–Thu for the exec audience · never edit within the first hour (algorithm penalty).

## 3. The Conversion Layer

Posts build audience; **comments and DMs build pipeline.** The protocol ([57 §3](57-founder-led-marketing.md)): every ICP-shaped commenter gets a substantive reply · pain-revealing replies get the soft-bridge DM within 24h · profile visitors from ICP companies (weekly review) get a connection note referencing the specific post they engaged: `Saw the eight-tab thread resonated — curious what your team's version looks like.` No pitch in the first message, ever. Target: 2–4 content-sourced discovery calls/week by launch ([57 KPIs](57-founder-led-marketing.md)).

**Outbound harmony:** sales outbound T1 ([37 Play 1](../07-sales/37-sales-playbook.md)) references the content ("the piece most CS leaders forward internally") — the feed warms the cold channel; never send outbound to someone mid-conversation in comments (crossing the streams reads as machinery).

## 4. What We Don't Do on LinkedIn

Polls (engagement junk) · "I'm humbled to announce" corporate voice · carousel-decks of recycled frameworks · tagging spree posts · AI-writing tells (em-dash storms, "delve," rhetorical triplets without content) — the audience that got burned by AI *notices AI-flavored writing about AI trust*; everything ships in the founder's actual voice or not at all.

**KPIs:** follower growth with ICP-share sampling (quality over count) · long-post average impressions trend · comment-to-post ratio (the resonance signal) · content-sourced discovery calls/week (the number) · DM protocol ≤ 24h compliance ≥ 90% ([73](../13-operations/73-marketing-metrics.md) dashboards).
