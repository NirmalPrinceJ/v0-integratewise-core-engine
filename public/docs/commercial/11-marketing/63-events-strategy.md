# 63 — Events Strategy

Status: CANONICAL · Commercial OS
Owner: Founder
Last verified: 2026-07-21

---

## Purpose

Which rooms are worth the founder's physical presence, what happens in them, and the follow-up system that converts hallway conversations into pipeline. Events are precision strikes ([50 §3](../09-gtm/50-gtm-strategy.md)) — 2–3 per year, chosen for ICP density, never booth theater.

**Target audience:** Founder; future marketing hire.

---

## 1. The Portfolio (2026–2027)

| Event | Why | Play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dreamforce** (annual) | The MuleSoft/Salesforce ecosystem home turf ([48 P1](../09-gtm/48-partner-strategy.md)); founder alumni credibility; the ICP's CRM universe in one building | No booth. 15–20 pre-booked meetings (ecosystem contacts + open pipeline + design-partner intros); ecosystem-party circuit; one campfire/community talk if secured |
| **CS-practitioner conferences** (1–2/yr — Gain Grow Retain-adjacent, Pulse-adjacent, or regional CS meetups) | P1/P2 personas concentrated; wound stories on stage everywhere | The talk (§2) if secured; otherwise strategic attendance: 10 pre-booked conversations, teardown-driven hallway presence |
| **Local/virtual CS & RevOps meetups** (monthly-opportunistic) | Cheap reps for the talk; ICP in listening mode | The 20-minute version of the flagship narrative ([61](61-webinar-strategy.md)) |
| Industry mega-conferences (SaaStr etc.) | Attend only with ≥ 8 pre-booked meetings; otherwise skip | — |

**The standing rule:** no sponsorships, no booths, no swag pre-Series A. A booth is a $15K way to stand still; the founder walking with a calendar full of pre-booked meetings outperforms it every time at our stage.

## 2. The Talk (the event asset, one narrative refined all year)

**Title:** *"The Human API: why your best people are your worst integration — and what AI actually changes."*
**Arc (20 or 40 min builds):** the $8M wound story ([14](../01-foundation/14-founder-story.md)) → the eight-tab teardown with live audience math → why bolted-on AI failed (the Four Enemies, the Quiet Demotion) → the principle stack (shared bench · owned truth · approval between) → one live moment: the approval + receipt, 90 seconds, from the phone if needed. No product tour; the talk sells the *category*, the hallway sells the product.
**The close:** never "visit our website" — always the question: *"What's your team's version of the eight-tab morning? Come tell me — I collect them."* (It works. They come. The collection becomes essays [56](56-content-strategy.md).)

## 3. The Event Operating System

**Before (2 weeks out):** target list from attendee/speaker rolls filtered by ICP ([33](../06-customers/33-ideal-customer-profile.md)) · outreach referencing something true ("your churn-postmortem post") · calendar goal: 60% booked before flying · LinkedIn "I'll be at X — collecting eight-tab mornings" post.
**During:** meetings > sessions, always · same-evening notes per conversation (voice memo standard: who, pain quote, why-now signal, next step) · the demo lives on the laptop, shown only when asked twice (scarcity posture).
**After (the week the ROI happens):** every conversation gets its specific follow-up within 72h — the thing promised (an essay, an intro, a POV conversation), never a generic deck · ICP conversations enter CRM as sourced pipeline ([74](../13-operations/74-sales-metrics.md)) · one "what I learned at X" essay banks the trip ([56 T4](56-content-strategy.md)).

## 4. Hosting (small, owned, deferred-but-designed)

**The Workbench Dinner** (post-launch, quarterly, one city): 8–10 CS/RevOps leaders, no presentation, one structured question ("what did AI actually change on your team this year?"), Chatham House rule. Cost ≈ $1.5K; yield: relationships no conference produces. First one gates on: 10 customers + launch done. Scales later into the community's annual moment ([62 §2](62-community-strategy.md)).

**KPIs:** pre-booked meeting rate ≥ 60% before travel · conversations → CRM-logged ≥ 90% · 72h follow-up compliance 100% · event-sourced pipeline per event ≥ 5 qualified conversations · cost per qualified conversation < $500 · talk acceptances/year (leading indicator of category traction).
