## Purpose

What we publish, why, and on what engine — a content system sized for a founder's four weekly hours that still compounds like a media operation. Content is our primary demand channel ([50 §3](../09-gtm/50-gtm-strategy.md)); this is its editorial constitution.

**Target audience:** Founder (writer), PMM hat (editor), future content hire.

---

## 1. Editorial Thesis

We publish one thing, in many forms: **the truth about work, AI, and trust — from someone who lived it.** Not product content with a thought-leadership costume. The reader is the Quiet Demotion demographic ([12 §3](../03-positioning-messaging/12-messaging-framework.md)): smart, burned, allergic to hype. They reward specificity, evidence, and the courage to name what failed — the same properties as the product.

**The five standing themes (canon-evolved):**

| Theme | The story | Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **T1 The Human API** | Fragmentation lived: mornings, handovers, the reconstruction tax | Weekly presence |
| **T2 The Quiet Demotion** | AI trust: what broke it, what restores it (approval, evidence, silence) | Weekly presence |
| **T3 The Category** | Work is leaving the apps; what a collaborative-work workspace is; the rubric | 2×/month |
| **T4 Build in the Open** | Design-partner learnings, honest metrics, the noisy-v1 → good-v4 journey | 2×/month |
| **T5 The Craft** | For technical readers: spine architecture, provenance, governed execution, MCP | Monthly |

## 2. The Engine (one essay, five surfaces)

**Weekly rhythm (4 founder-hours):**

```
Mon (2h)   Write the week's essay (800–1,500 words, one theme, one target query [55 Ring 2])
Tue (30m)  Publish: site (canonical) → newsletter [60] → LinkedIn native adaptation [59]
Wed–Fri (3×20m)  Derivative posts: one sharp excerpt/day on LinkedIn; comments answered
Fri (30m)  Log performance; bank reader replies as future essay seeds
```

Everything else — webinar talks ([61](61-webinar-strategy.md)), event talks ([63](63-events-strategy.md)), sales-sequence assets ([37 Play 1](../07-sales/37-sales-playbook.md)) — is *recycled essays*, never net-new writing. The essay is the atom; the system is the reactor.

## 3. Format Library (complete, reusable)

| Format | Structure | Example title |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Wound Story** | Anecdote (real, anonymized) → the structural cause → the principle → soft bridge | "The $8M account nobody saw going red" |
| **The Demotion Essay** | Reader's AI disappointment mirrored → why it was *correct* → what would have to be true → the rubric | "You were right to demote your AI" |
| **The Contrarian Take** | Popular claim → why it fails operationally → our counter-principle | "Autonomous agents are a governance incident with a roadmap" |
| **The Teardown** | A work ritual dissected minute-by-minute → the tax computed → the alternative | "The eight-tab morning, itemized" |
| **The Build Log** | What we tried → what broke → what we learned → honest numbers | "Our signal engine was noisy for three versions. Here's v4." |
| **The Explainer** | One concept ([22](../04-product-marketing/22-adaptive-spine-story.md)–[27](../04-product-marketing/27-governance-story.md) stories rendered) → why it exists → proof beat | "Why our AI is designed to stay silent" |

## 4. Standards (the editorial bar)

Every piece: names a failure before a feature ([20 §2](../04-product-marketing/20-product-marketing-guide.md)) · contains ≥ 1 number, mechanism, or evidence artifact · passes the terminology checklist ([18 §8](../03-positioning-messaging/18-terminology-guide.md)) · written in the voice ([13 §2](../01-foundation/13-brand-story.md)): calm, precise, on the human's side · zero hype-words (revolutionize, game-changing, unleash — auto-reject list) · product mentions earn their place or get cut; T1/T2 essays may mention the product only in the final paragraph.

**The credibility rule:** we publish honest numbers including unflattering ones (build logs showing failure). One inflated claim costs the entire demoted-AI audience — they've been lied to before; that's *why* they're our audience.

## 5. Distribution & Compounding

Primary: LinkedIn ([59](59-linkedin-strategy.md)) + newsletter ([60](60-newsletter-strategy.md)) — where the ICP already is. Secondary: HN for T5 craft pieces (technical honesty travels there); community reposts ([62](62-community-strategy.md)). Each quarter: the best 12 essays get audited — top performers become pillar pages ([55](../10-website/55-seo-strategy.md)), webinar topics, and event talks; the corpus is the future book/keynote, deliberately.

**KPIs ([73](../13-operations/73-marketing-metrics.md) owns dashboards):** essays shipped/weeks elapsed ≥ 90% (consistency is the strategy) · subscriber growth ≥ 10%/mo · essay → site CTA rate · content-attributed pipeline (first-touch) · reader-reply rate (the leading indicator of resonance — replies become discovery calls).
