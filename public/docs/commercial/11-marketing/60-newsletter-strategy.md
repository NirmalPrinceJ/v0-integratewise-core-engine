# 60 — Newsletter Strategy

Status: CANONICAL · Commercial OS
Owner: Founder
Last verified: 2026-07-21

---

## Purpose

The owned-audience asset: the one channel no algorithm can take away, where the demoted-AI audience warms slowly toward a POV. LinkedIn rents attention; the newsletter owns it.

**Target audience:** Founder (writer/operator); future marketing hire.

---

## 1. The Publication

**Name:** ***The Workbench*** — a weekly letter on work, AI, and trust.
**Promise (subscribe-page copy):** *"One essay a week on why work got scattered, why AI got demoted, and how humans and AI actually work together. From a former MuleSoft architect and CSM who lived both problems. No hype. Numbers included."*
**Cadence:** weekly, Tuesday morning ET (the essay from Monday's write session [56 §2](56-content-strategy.md), newsletter-formatted).
**Platform:** any owned-list provider with export (the list is the asset; portability is doctrine — practice what we preach).

## 2. Issue Anatomy (fixed, 5-minute read)

```
1. The essay (the week's piece, full text — never a teaser link; respect the inbox)
2. One number (a single stat with one line of context — design-partner metric, market fact from [06], or build-log figure)
3. One thing we learned (build-in-the-open: a sentence or two, honest)
4. The quiet PS (rotating, one line: POV page / demo film / a reader-reply question)
```

No link roundups (undifferentiated), no product changelogs (wrong audience contract), no sponsorships (credibility is the product).

## 3. Growth System (compounding, not campaigns)

**Sources in expected order of volume:** LinkedIn posts' standing CTA ("I write about ending this weekly") · manifesto page subscribe ([54 LP-1](../10-website/54-landing-pages.md), target ≥ 8%) · website footer + essay-page subscribe blocks · webinar registrations (consent-checked, [61](61-webinar-strategy.md)) · event QR ([63](63-events-strategy.md)) · launch spike ([51 §4](../09-gtm/51-launch-strategy.md): +500 in 48h, +1,500 in 30d).

**The reply loop (the newsletter's secret job):** every issue ends with a real question; every reader reply gets a founder response within 48h. Replies are: essay seeds ([56 §2](56-content-strategy.md) Fri ritual) · discovery-call candidates (the soft bridge, [57 §3](57-founder-led-marketing.md)) · the qualitative resonance signal no open-rate provides. A 2,000-subscriber list that replies beats a 20,000 list that doesn't.

## 4. List Hygiene & Standards

Double opt-in (deliverability + the trust posture) · quarterly sunset flow for 90-day non-openers ("still want this?" → clean list, honest metrics) · no purchased lists, no scraped adds, ever · every issue passes the terminology check ([18 §8](../03-positioning-messaging/18-terminology-guide.md)) · subscriber data handled per our own security story ([42](../07-sales/42-security-faq.md)) — we are Customer Zero for data respect too.

## 5. The Long Game

The newsletter is the company's compounding asset with three future payoffs, planned now: the launch's owned audience (Layer 1 primes, Layer 2 converts [51](../09-gtm/51-launch-strategy.md)) · the Series A narrative's demand evidence ("N thousand ICP readers, X% reply rate" — [68](../12-corporate/68-fundraising-story.md)) · the community seed ([62](62-community-strategy.md) launches *from* the most-engaged repliers, not from zero).

**KPIs:** net subscriber growth ≥ 10%/mo · open rate ≥ 45% (owned-audience quality bar) · reply rate ≥ 1% per issue (the metric that matters) · newsletter-sourced discovery calls ([73](../13-operations/73-marketing-metrics.md)) · list-health: < 30% 90-day-inactive.
