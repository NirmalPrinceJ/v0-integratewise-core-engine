## Purpose

The roadmap as narrative: what we tell customers, prospects, analysts, and investors about where the product goes — themed, honest, and commitment-disciplined. A startup roadmap conversation is a trust exercise; this document keeps us trustworthy.

**Target audience:** Sales/CS (roadmap questions in deals), founder (briefings), analysts and Enterprise buyers (under NDA).

---

## 1. The Roadmap Grammar (themes, not features; sequence, not dates)

Public roadmap talk uses four standing themes with a strict *now / next / later* grammar — never dated feature lists (dates we miss cost trust; themes we advance build it):

| Theme | Now (shipping/hardening) | Next | Later |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Deeper benches** (the wedge, perfected) | CS workbench end-to-end on HubSpot; signal quality iteration (v1 noisy → v4 good, said openly) | Salesforce parity; meeting/QBR prep depth | Vertical bench variants |
| **More benches** (the expansion architecture [46 V3](../08-customer-success/46-expansion-strategy.md)) | Sales + RevOps benches in design-partner use | Exec bench; Finance/Service compositions | Custom bench composition tooling |
| **Deeper trust** (the governance lead, extended) | TruthLayer + approval lifecycle GA-hard; audit polish | SSO/SCIM, policy customization (Enterprise tier [30](../05-value-pricing/30-pricing-strategy.md)); SOC 2 milestones ([42 §3](../07-sales/42-security-faq.md)) | Region pinning; advanced policy authoring |
| **Wider fabric** (connected capability) | 70+ connectors; governed writebacks on the wedge stack | Marketplace listings ([49](../09-gtm/49-marketplace-strategy.md)); handoff adapters (n8n/Zapier) | IW-Continuity-Bridge GA — *gated by distribution doctrine, stated exactly that way* |

**The narrative through-line (say it in every roadmap conversation):** *"Everything on this roadmap deepens one loop — humans and Twins doing governed work over owned truth. We don't add adjacent products; we make the loop wider and deeper."* This is also the internal triage test ([44 §5](../08-customer-success/44-customer-success-playbook.md) feedback routing): a request that doesn't deepen the loop is a no, kindly.

## 2. Commitment Discipline (the rules that keep trust)

1. **Three tiers of language, used precisely:** *shipped* (demo it) · *building* (in design-partner hands, honest state) · *exploring* (no commitment implied — say "exploring").
2. **Dates only in contracts, only for items we control** ([32 §4 / 43](../05-value-pricing/32-licensing-strategy.md)): scoped Enterprise commitments, nothing else.
3. **No roadmap promises to close a deal.** A deal that needs a promised feature is a deal at the wrong time — offer the design-partner path instead (influence, not promises).
4. **The public changelog is the roadmap's receipt:** launches per PMM tiering ([20 §3](../04-product-marketing/20-product-marketing-guide.md)); build-logs tell the honest journey ([56 T4](../11-marketing/56-content-strategy.md)).
5. **Roadmap regret protocol:** when we kill or delay something we discussed, affected customers hear it from us first, with the why. ([47 no-surprise rule](../08-customer-success/47-renewal-strategy.md) applies to product too.)

## 3. Audience Renderings

| Audience | What they get |
| --- | --- |
| Prospect (in-deal) | The themes table verbatim + "which theme matters most to you?" (discovery, not promising) |
| Customer (QBR) | Themes + their requests' honest status + you-said-we-shipped notes ([44 §5](../08-customer-success/44-customer-success-playbook.md)) |
| Enterprise buyer | NDA roadmap session: next-tier depth on Deeper Trust theme; contractual items separated explicitly |
| Analyst ([66](66-analyst-relations.md)) | Themes + the honest slide (what's unproven) — candor is the AR strategy |
| Investor ([67 §8](67-investor-narrative.md)) | Themes mapped to milestones the raise buys; the build plan's 180-day spine |

**KPIs:** zero missed public date commitments (by having ~zero public dates) · roadmap-promise deals = 0 · you-said-we-shipped notes delivered each quarter to every design partner · theme advancement demonstrated in every QBR cycle.
