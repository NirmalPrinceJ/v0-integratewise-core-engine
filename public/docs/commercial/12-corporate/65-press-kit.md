## 6. Statement Templates (pre-drafted for speed)

**Incident/security statement skeleton:** what happened (facts only, no speculation) → impact scope (tenants affected/not) → what fail-closed architecture contained → actions underway → commitment + next update time. Rule: publish within 4 hours of confirmed customer impact; accuracy > completeness; never "sophisticated attack" theater. ([42 §5](../07-sales/42-security-faq.md) posture.)

**Industry-AI-incident comment (when a rival's agent makes news):** empathy first, no gloating → the structural point (ungoverned autonomy fails predictably) → our design principle, one line → decline further comment on the specific vendor. ([07 T4 scenario](../02-category-market/07-industry-landscape.md).)

**Funding/launch templates:** headline + boilerplate + founder quote slot + one customer/design-partner quote slot + fact sheet link. Pre-approved structure so news days are assembly, not authorship.

**KPIs:** press-page completeness (all assets current ≤ 90 days) · coverage accuracy (our category words used — the [51 §4](../09-gtm/51-launch-strategy.md) pickup metric) · response time to press inquiries < 24h · zero statements issued off-template in a crisis.
