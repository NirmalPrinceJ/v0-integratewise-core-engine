## 1. Commercial Terms

**"How is it priced?"** Per named-user seat, per month, billed annually (monthly available +20%). Tiers: Starter $19 · Growth $59 · Command $119 · Enterprise custom (15-seat minimum). A seat = one person + their AI Twin — the Twin is never a separate line item.

**"What's a named seat? Can we share logins?"** One person's working identity. No shared/concurrent seats — approval attribution and personal Twin scope require identity (a governance feature, not a revenue rule). Reassignment when someone leaves: free, immediate.

**"Mid-term seat changes?"** Add anytime at contract rate with quarterly true-up (no retroactive penalties). Reduce at renewal; Enterprise agreements can include a ±10% flex band.

**"Price protection?"** Renewal increases capped at max(7%, CPI) with 60-day notice. Multi-year: year-2 price lock free; 2-year prepay −10%.

**"What discounts exist?"** Volume (50+ seats −10%, 100+ −15%), design-partner terms (2026 cohort, −50% year 1 with case-study rights), nonprofit/edu −30%. We hold list otherwise — predictable pricing is part of the trust posture.

**"Payment terms?"** Net-30, annual upfront. Card or invoice/ACH.

## 2. Evaluation & Onboarding

**"Free trial?"** Better: a 30-day **proof of value** — up to 10 seats, full capability, your real tools, three success criteria agreed in writing, decision date attached. No auto-conversion; it ends with a decision, not a surprise invoice.

**"Implementation costs? Services?"** None. Connectors are OAuth self-serve (minutes each); first value same-day; onboarding is included ([45](../08-customer-success/45-onboarding-playbook.md)). No implementation partner required — total cost is the subscription ([29 §3 TCO box](../05-value-pricing/29-roi-framework.md)).

**"Training?"** The user surface is four controls (Store in Spine, Ask/Assign/Approve Twin). One enablement session included; materials provided.

## 3. Legal & Risk

**"Whose paper?"** Ours by default (Order Form + MSA + DPA; Enterprise adds SLA + Security Addendum). Customer paper considered at 100+ seats with our non-negotiables preserved.

**"Who owns the data?"** You do — including derived Spine content. Our processing license is service-provision only. **No training of foundation models on your data. No cross-tenant use.** On exit: export anytime (open formats), 60-day retrieval, certified deletion.

**"Liability?"** Capped at 12 months of fees, with standard carve-outs (confidentiality, data-protection breach, IP indemnity). IP indemnification included.

**"SLA?"** Enterprise: 99.9% with service credits. Other tiers: published status page, best-efforts. We do not contract SLAs we haven't measured — you'll find we under-promise deliberately.

**"Insurance?"** Standard commercial stack appropriate to stage (details in security packet); certificates on request.

**"Vendor lock-in?"** Structurally minimized: your tools of record keep operating (we assemble, not migrate); Spine exports open-format anytime; AI providers are swappable by configuration. Our retention strategy is compounding value, not exit friction — it's in the contract ([32 §2](../05-value-pricing/32-licensing-strategy.md)).

## 4. Company Diligence (the startup questions, answered straight)

**"You're early — why should we depend on you?"** Three structural mitigations before any promises: (1) your systems of record are untouched — worst case you lose a layer, not your operations; (2) your Spine exports — your assembled truth leaves with you; (3) design-partner terms compensate the risk with pricing and roadmap influence. And one promise: honest status, always — see our certification table with dates, not adjectives ([42 §3](42-security-faq.md)).

**"References?"** Design-partner references as the cohort matures; until then we offer the deeper substitute: a POV on your own accounts. Evidence in your tenant beats testimony from someone else's.

**"Roadmap commitments?"** Directional roadmap shared under NDA ([69](../12-corporate/69-product-roadmap-story.md)); contractual commitments only for scoped Enterprise items with dates we control.
