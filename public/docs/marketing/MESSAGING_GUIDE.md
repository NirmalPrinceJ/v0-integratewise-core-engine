## Product description

IntegrateWise is an AI work partner for teams that are tired of jumping between tools to understand what is happening. It reads across the systems you already use, pulls together the right context, remembers the thread of work, notices changes that matter, and prepares grounded drafts. It never acts without your approval.

## The daily-job story

### Morning

Instead of checking email, chat, CRM, notes, and support before starting, the user sees what changed overnight, what needs attention, and the thread they were working on.

### During work

Instead of pulling information from several places for a meeting, escalation, or decision, the user opens the work item and sees the relevant context already assembled.

### When something changes

Instead of discovering a problem late, the user gets a quiet, grounded signal: what changed, why it matters, and the source context behind it.

### When follow-through is needed

Instead of starting from a blank document, the user reviews a draft based on the actual account, project, or issue. They edit and approve it before anything happens.

### When returning

Instead of reconstructing the thread, the user picks up where they left off and sees what changed while they were away.

## AI contract

AI **reads, connects, remembers, notices, organizes, drafts, and tracks**.

AI does **not** send, change, decide, override the user, or act without approval.

## Discovery questions

- How long does it take to get ready for an important customer, project, or operational decision?
- Which tools do you open before you can answer a basic status question?
- What falls through because the follow-up, context, or signal lives somewhere else?
- What work do your best people repeat every day that should already be prepared?
- What would make you say, “I cannot go back to the old way of working”?

## Demo language

Show a real work item—not a feature menu.

1. “Here is what changed since you last looked.”
2. “Here is everything you need for this account in one place.”
3. “Here is a signal worth knowing, with the evidence behind it.”
4. “Ask for the outcome you need; the first draft is prepared from your real context.”
5. “Nothing happens until you review and approve it.”

## Words to use

Ready, prepared, connected, context, change, attention, signal, draft, follow-through, control, grounded, daily work.

## Words to avoid

Operational continuity, digital memory, canonical spine, capability fabric, execution plane, provider independence, agentic autonomy, workflow orchestration, HITL.
