# The Pitch

**Truth you own. AI you rent. Approval in between.**

---

## What IntegrateWise Is

IntegrateWise is a connected operational workbench with a persistent AI Twin that stays on the job.

The user's daily work already happens across many tools—CRM, email, projects, messaging, docs, tickets. Right now, the job of _coordinating_ that work is manual, repetitive, and exhausting even before the actual work begins. IntegrateWise replaces that coordination cycle with one place to work and an AI Twin that carries the context from task to task, day to day.

---

## The Four Promises

Your work is already in context.  
Your Twin remembers the work.  
AI does the hard lifting.  
You stay in control.

These are not new messages taken in isolation. Every IntegrateWise capability exists to make those four promise-words reliable, every day, across every connected department.

## The Problem We Solve

People already know how to do the job. The problem is everything they have to do around the job.

Every morning starts with the same cycle: find the customer file, check the notes, locate the last conversation, catch up on changes, compare information across systems, and by the time the real work begins, energy is already spent. Then they open an AI assistant, explain the situation again, and start from zero.

Most AI today is a blank conversation that forgets context the moment a session ends, relies on the user to gather the same information AI was supposed to help with, and offers no reliable continuity from yesterday to today.

IntegrateWise ends that exact cycle.

## What Makes It Different

### Continuity, not chat.

A typical AI assistant starts from blank context every session. Your IntegrateWise Twin remembers the work you allowed it to remember. The next day does not begin from zero.

### Memory you own, memory it rents.

Twin memory exists only where the user has allowed it to develop. The Twin does not drift away from operational reality. Canonical operational truth remains governed, owned, and controlled by you.

### AI that prepares, not acts.

The Twin prepares the work. It drafts, summarizes, compares, researches, and flags changes. Before anything consequential is sent, updated, or changed in your systems, you review and approve. That is the difference between help and an unpoliced background process.

## The Four-Button Model

Users do not need to understand engines, pipelines, or architecture. They interact through four simple product actions:

- **Store in Spine** — keep something as part of the work
- **Ask Your Twin** — understand a situation without rereading everything yourself
- **Assign Your Twin** — give the hard preparation work to AI
- **Approve Twin's Action** — review, decide, and approve when something should happen

Behind those four buttons is a governed flow that keeps AI useful and human control non-negotiable. The product language is simple. The responsibility model is simple. The protection model is simple.

## Who It Is For

IntegrateWise is built for any role whose work depends on connected operational systems. Customer Success sees account details, support history, renewals, and commitments in one place and asks the Twin to prepare the next conversation. Engineering sees issues, incidents, dependencies, and current decisions, and asks the Twin to explain what changed. Finance sees invoices, vendors, approvals, and cash-related work. Founders see customers, revenue, product, hiring, and decisions together. Each person sees only the work relevant to their role.

## The Market Position

Enterprises have spent decades integrating best-of-breed systems. The result is connected data and disconnected workforces. IntegrateWise is the ~governed~ layer above those integrations: a shared operational workbench for every department, governed by human approval, powered by a persistent AI Twin that understands continuity.

We do not replace specialist tools. We coordinate around them.
