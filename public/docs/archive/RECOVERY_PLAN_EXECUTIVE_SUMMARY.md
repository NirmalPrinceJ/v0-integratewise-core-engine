## The Problem

The product drifted from **human-centric** (January 2025) to **AI-centric** (current state).

**What changed:**
- January: "Stop Being Human APIs" (human workbench solving fragmentation)
- Current: "Multi-model AI orchestration" (infrastructure solving infrastructure)

**Why it matters:**
- Human problem = "I work across 10 disconnected tools. Context switching costs me 3 hours/day."
- AI infrastructure = solves technical challenges, not human ones
- Customers don't buy infrastructure. They buy relief.

---

## The Solution (Three Layers)

### Layer 1: Restore Human Workbench (PRIMARY)
- Dashboard (overview, quick actions)
- Contacts, Companies, Opportunities
- Tasks, Calendar, Notes, Meetings
- Communications, Documents, Knowledge
- Goals, Values, Reports, Settings

**Why:** This is what people spend 8 hours/day in. This is the real product.

### Layer 2: AI Gets Its Own Home (SEPARATE)
- Observations (what Twin sees)
- Memory (what Twin learns)
- Routines (what Twin does daily)
- Collaboration (how human and AI work together)

**Why:** Twin isn't idle. It has daily responsibilities. But it doesn't interrupt human work.

### Layer 3: Continuity Engine (ALWAYS INTEGRATEWISE)
- Identity (Auth, Org, Teams, Roles)
- Spine Schema (Canonical truth)
- Shared Memory (with boundaries)
- Governance (policies, access)

**Why:** This is the actual moat. This is invisible infrastructure that makes everything work.
