## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

IntegrateWise is building the IW Continuity Bridge — the Operational Continuity Platform that turns raw operational signals into canonical operational memory, allows intelligence to reason over that memory, proposes governed actions, executes approved actions, and preserves continuity.

**The core thesis:** Every business tool today is an island. IntegrateWise is the bridge that connects them all through a single Spine of canonical truth, enabling governed AI-powered continuity across every operational surface.

**Stage:** Pre-seed / Seed
**Target market:** SMBs and mid-market enterprises (10-500 employees)
**Revenue model:** SaaS (per-seat + usage-based components)
**Headquarters:** India (cost advantage for global distribution)

## 4. VALUATION METHODOLOGY

### 4.1 What Determines Seed Valuation

**Primary factors (60% weight):**

1. **Team quality** — Technical depth, domain expertise, execution track record
2. **Market size** — TAM/SAM/SOM for operational continuity platform
3. **Product differentiation** — Spine architecture, governed AI, Cloudflare-native
4. **Traction** — Revenue, users, connected systems, operational objects

**Secondary factors (40% weight):** 5. **Technology moat** — Proprietary architecture, data network effects 6. **Go-to-market strategy** — Channel partnerships, content strategy, community 7. **Competitive landscape** — Positioning vs existing tools 8. **Founder-market fit** — Why this founder, why now

### 4.2 Premium Factors (+10-20% above baseline)

| Premium Factor                          | Impact  | Evidence                                |
| --------------------------------------- | ------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Technical founder with domain expertise | +10-15% | Track record, architecture depth        |
| Early revenue/traction                  | +10-20% | MRR, customer logos, growth rate        |
| Strong technical moat                   | +5-15%  | Patents, proprietary tech, data effects |
| Cloudflare ecosystem advantage          | +5-10%  | Infrastructure efficiency, scalability  |
| India cost advantage                    | +5-10%  | Runway multiplier, talent quality       |
| Existing customer relationships         | +10-15% | Warm intros, pilot customers            |

### 4.3 Discount Factors (-10-20% below baseline)

| Discount Factor             | Impact  | Mitigation                      |
| --------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------- |
| Pre-revenue                 | -15-20% | Show pipeline, pilot interest   |
| Solo founder risk           | -10-15% | Technical co-founder search     |
| Competitive market          | -5-10%  | Differentiation narrative       |
| Early stage (pre-product)   | -10-15% | Strong demo, architecture proof |
| Geographic discount (India) | -5-10%  | Global-first positioning        |

### 4.4 Recommended Raise: ₹5.5Cr at ₹30Cr Pre-money

**Rationale:**

- 15% dilution at seed (healthy range: 10-20%)
- ₹5.5Cr provides 24-month runway at ₹5-6Cr Scenario burn
- ₹30Cr pre-money = ₹35.5Cr post-money
- Valuation justified by:
  - Technical architecture depth (Spine, governed AI, Cloudflare-native)
  - Market opportunity ($50B+ operational tools market)
  - India cost advantage (3-4x runway multiplier)
  - Founder expertise in continuity systems
  - Early customer interest (pre-sales pipeline)

## 7. PUSHBACK RESPONSES

### 7.1 "Why not raise in the US?"

"We're building a global product, not a US product. Cloudflare infrastructure is location-agnostic. India gives us 3-4x runway multiplier with the same talent quality. US investors get global exposure at Indian cost efficiency — this is arbitrage, not compromise."

### 7.2 "India market is different from US market"

"We're not targeting the India market. We're targeting the global operational tools market ($50B+) from India. Our first customers will be US/EU companies who need operational continuity. India is our cost center, not our market."

### 7.3 "Pre-revenue is risky"

"Pre-revenue is standard at seed. We're de-risking with: (1) Customer Zero activation in progress, (2) 5+ pilot conversations, (3) Architecture that's 80% built, (4) Founder with deep domain expertise. We're not asking for faith — we're asking for runway to prove it."

### 7.4 "Cloudflare is expensive at scale"

"Cloudflare's pricing is competitive at our scale. D1 is free up to 5GB, KV is free up to 100K reads/day, R2 has no egress fees. Our infrastructure cost is $5-10K/month at Customer Zero, scaling to $20K/month at 50 customers. That's $240K/year — well within our burn rate."

### 7.5 "Solo founder risk"

"We're actively searching for a technical co-founder. We have a clear profile, equity structure, and investment agreement ready. The co-founder search is parallel to the fundraise — we expect to close both within 90 days."
