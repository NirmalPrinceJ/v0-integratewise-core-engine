## Engagement Protocol

### Comment Protocol
- Answer every substantive comment within **24 hours**.
- Pain-revealing replies get the soft-bridge DM within **24 hours**:
  > "That eight-tab morning you described — that's literally what we're building against. Want to see what we've got? No pitch, 20 minutes."
- Profile visitors from ICP companies (weekly review) get a connection note referencing the specific post they engaged.
- **Target:** 2–4 content-sourced discovery calls this week.

### Ratio Discipline
- ≥ 3 useful comments elsewhere per 1 post of our own.
- Show up as the person who understands the problem, not the vendor circling it.

## Cross-Posting Rules

- LinkedIn long-post is a **native rewrite**, not a link dump.
- Links go in the **first comment**, not in the post body.
- **0–1 hashtags** (they're dead weight).
- **Never edit within the first hour** (algorithm penalty).
- **No engagement bait**, no competitor dunking, no AI-writing tells.

## Amplification

- **Sales outbound (T1 sequence)** references the piece: "the post most CS leaders forward internally."
- **Never send outbound** to someone mid-conversation in comments (crossing the streams reads as machinery).
