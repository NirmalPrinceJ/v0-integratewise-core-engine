# IntegrateWise Stakeholder Pitch

## One-line positioning

**IntegrateWise makes daily work lighter by connecting the tools a team already uses and having the hard lifting done before people start their day.**

## The pain in a buyer’s words

“I opened five tools to answer one question. I know something changed, but I cannot remember where I saw it. I lost the thread of my work when a fire drill pulled me away. I spend too much time gathering context and too little time making the call.”

This is not a lack-of-tools problem. It is the daily cost of being the bridge between too many tools.

## The answer

IntegrateWise gives people one place to begin. It shows what changed, what needs attention, and where they left off. It assembles the context behind the work, notices meaningful signals, and prepares grounded drafts.

The AI does the lifting around the decision. The person makes the decision. Nothing is sent, changed, or run without explicit approval.

## What customers buy

- Less time spent searching, reconciling, and rebuilding context.
- A better-prepared start to every task, meeting, and decision.
- A clear view of what changed and what matters now.
- Drafts and follow-through grounded in the actual work.
- AI help that is useful without taking control away from the user.

## Sales conversation

1. Start with the last time the buyer had to open multiple tools to prepare for one task.
2. Show the morning view: what changed, what needs attention, and where the user left off.
3. Open one real work item and show its assembled context.
4. Show a quiet signal with supporting evidence.
5. Assign a specific outcome, such as a renewal brief or follow-up draft, and review it before it goes anywhere.

## What not to lead with

Do not sell operational continuity, memory architecture, governance, a capability fabric, provider independence, or AI models. These are implementation strengths—not the customer’s desired outcome.

## The close

**Your work, ready for you. The hard lifting, handled. You stay in control.**
