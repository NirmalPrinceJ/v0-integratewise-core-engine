## The Product Promise

**IntegrateWise removes the daily cycle of context reconstruction.**

1. Your work is already in context.
2. Your Twin remembers the work.
3. AI does the hard lifting.
4. You stay in control.

## Frozen Laws

- **Twin Law**: Twin is a capability of the Workbench, not a panel.
- **Context Law**: The human never explains already-known work context to AI.
- **Memory Law**: Spine owns truth. Memory informs truth. Memory is not truth.
- **Authority Law**: Confidence increases trust. Confidence never grants authority.
- **Silence Law**: Twin prefers silence over low-value commentary.
- **Capability Law**: Workbench projects tool capabilities, doesn't duplicate tools.
- **Bridge Law**: One platform. One boundary. Different projections.
- **Product–Platform Law**: Broad underneath. Narrow at the surface.
