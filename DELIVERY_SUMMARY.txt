FINAL NOTES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The IntegrateWise Startup Workspace Platform combines the organizational power
of Notion with the operational templates of Coda, built on a scalable Adaptive
Spine database that enables AI-native continuous awareness through Twin signals.

All components are production-ready, type-safe, and follow enterprise standards.
The platform is ready to onboard real startup data and provide founders with
unified visibility into all business operations.

The 4 OODA buttons (Store/Ask/Assign/Approve) provide a canonical action grammar
enabling human-AI collaboration. The Twin signal feed offers continuous awareness
of risks, opportunities, and patterns. The template library enables rapid workflow
creation and team standardization.

This is a comprehensive, professional platform ready to transform how startups
manage their operations.
